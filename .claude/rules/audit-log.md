# The audit log's period and text (#393)

The audit log filters by a period and by a text beside its type, author and account, through `from`, `to` and `text` in the URL, read by `auditLogFiltersOf` with the shared `dayParamOf` and `textParamOf` of `src/ui/filter-params.ts`. A day is `yyyy-mm-dd`, a calendar day from 2015 to 2099, and a text is trimmed and holds 512 characters at most, the length of a reason (ADR 0004); anything else, empty, blank, repeated, a day that does not exist or a text that is too long, counts as absent (`src/audit-log/read-audit-log-period-and-text.test.ts`). The URL writes them in the order of the form's fields, `type`, `author`, `account`, `text`, `from`, `to`, which `auditLogHref`, the Sheet's form and the page's form all give (`e2e/audit-log.test.ts`), and a submit of the form leaves the fields it holds empty in the address, which count as absent. Both filters work in memory, on what Discord's audit log gives, as the author's and the account's do, so that they count toward the 50 changes of a page and the read stops only once it holds enough of what they keep (`CLAUDE.md`, **Navigation**). A period reads from the newest entry in every case: the cursor and the stop do not use the period's bounds, which would spare Discord some pages and was left out, #393 asking for the filtering in memory (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #393, for Gilles to review).

## The period

- It runs from the first midnight of its first day of Paris to the last midnight of its last, both days included (`periodMatcher`, in `audit-log-period.ts`, from `parisDayStart` and `parisDayAfter`), on the moment that the entry's ID holds, as the page groups by day.
- A change of action writes two entries a moment apart (`CLAUDE.md`, **Write results**), and the page joins them into one line dated by the first, the entering one. The entries of such a change are kept with 60 s of tolerance beyond the period's edges, the join's own window, `JOINING_WINDOW_MS`, before they are joined, and the joined line is then checked against the period itself, so that a change whose two writes fall on either side of Paris midnight shows once, whole, under the day of its first write, whichever edge of a period the midnight is (`src/audit-log/read-audit-log-action-changes.test.ts`). Without the tolerance, the half that falls inside the period would show alone, as an addition or a removal (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #393, for Gilles to review).

## Beyond what Discord keeps

- Discord keeps 45 days of audit log (ADR 0004), and `periodReachOf` says where a period stands against them, from the clock at the read, which the tests freeze: `starts-before-retention` when its first day is before the Paris day of 45 days ago, `outside-retention` when its last day ends before that moment or its first day starts in the future, and `reversed` when its first day is after its last, which wins over the other two.
- The page says so in a sentence each. When the period starts before, « Seuls les 45 derniers jours sont lus : Discord ne garde pas le journal d’audit plus longtemps, et la période commence avant. » shows above the changes, on a page that holds some as on one that holds none, since the period is read down to what Discord keeps. When it lies outside or in the future, « Cette période n’est pas dans les 45 derniers jours, les seuls que le journal d’audit de Discord garde. » takes the empty page's place, and when it is reversed, « Le premier jour de la période est après son dernier jour : aucun changement ne peut y correspondre. », each over « Réinitialiser » (`e2e/audit-log.test.ts`).
- The last two read nothing from Discord (`readsNothing`), since nothing can match, where the first reads as any other period. #393 asks only that a period outside the 45 days say so, and the orchestrator of #381's run extended that to a period that starts before and to a reversed one; the wording is the agent's (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #393, for Gilles to review).

## The text

- A text is contained in what a row shows as its reason or its word (`textMatcher`, in `audit-log-filters.ts`), whatever its case, through `toLocaleLowerCase`, and not whatever its accents, as the blocked words' filter and the search of accounts compare (#229, #324), so that « repet » does not find « répétées ». Any Unicode space, the no-break spaces of « « foo » » included, reads as an ordinary space on both sides, so that a text typed with plain spaces finds a reason that holds U+00A0.
- What a row shows as a reason, kind by kind: for a blocked word changed through the panel, its line, which holds the word and the name of its action, so that « nitro » finds « Ajout de « free nitro », action « Bloquer seulement » » and so does « bloquer »; for a word that changed action, the line of the two entries joined, « passe de … à … », or, for an entry left alone by a cut change, the addition or the removal it shows; the reason of a timeout, a kick, a ban or a lifting, typed through the panel or in Discord; and the name of the AutoMod rule that gave an automatic timeout, which the line shows in place of a reason. A change that a member made to the panel's rule in Discord shows « Règle modifiée dans Discord », no word and no reason, so that no text finds it, and a change with no reason is found by none. The text searches neither the names of the author and of the account, which their own filters take, nor the title of a line. Searching the word of a change made in Discord would need the entry's keyword changes, which the page does not read (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #393, for Gilles to review, #393 saying only « le motif ou le mot »).
- It filters in two stages. Before the join, `retainerOf` keeps each entry whose line may match, which for the two entries of a change of action is the reason they share and the addition and the removal that either shows alone, `mayShowAs`, so that both are kept or both dropped and the join can pair them. After the join, `lineRetainerOf` keeps the lines whose own text, as shown, holds the text: a joined line by its « passe de », an entry left alone by its addition or its removal. The first stage alone found « Ajout de … » and « Retrait de … » for a word that moved from one action to another, lines the unfiltered log does not show (`read-audit-log-action-changes.test.ts`).
- Known limit: a text that only the wording « Ajout de » or « Retrait de » finds keeps the two entries of every change of action at the first stage, which the second drops, so that a read with more than 50 changes of action in the 45 days can stop before the older lines that show, and miss an older page (inferred from the code, not tried).

## The fields

- « Auteur », « Compte », « Texte », « Du » and « Au » sit in the page's form and in its Sheet in that order, each with the basis of 11 rem that `FILTER_FIELD_CLASSES` of `audit-log-layout.ts` gives, so that three share a row at 768 and 1,024 px, where the period's two days share the second row with the button, four at 1,280 px, and the five and the button at 1,500 px, in the box of 1,080 px (a Playwright screenshot at each of the four widths, 2026-11-06). That constant equals the support tickets' (`ticket-archive-filter-layout.ts`) (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #393, for Gilles to review).
- The fold's button and its Sheet are named « Filtres » since #393, where #359 named them « Auteur et compte », which no longer describes five fields; `/records` and `/support-tickets` name theirs the same (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #393, for Gilles to review).
- `dayParamOf`, `textParamOf`, `parisDayStart`, `parisDayAfter`, `FilterFields`, `PERIOD_FIELDS`, `searchTextField`, `filterChipsOf` and `formatDay` are in `src/ui/`, so that the other logs of #381 read their period and their text the same way. `dayParamOf` and the two Paris day bounds moved there from `src/ticket-archives/`, whose filters import them and change in nothing else; the rest is new (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #393, for Gilles to review).

## The end-to-end flows

- At 360 px the five fields fold behind « Filtres », each at 44 px, the Sheet sends the address of the inline form, each chip removes its filter alone, and the staff member logs out from the bar's menu with the twelve controls of the filters in view, the five pills, « Réinitialiser », the button and the five chips, none prefetched. At 768 and 1,024 px the five fields are in the page's form, with no button and no chip, and send the same address.
- A flow at the default width keeps the changes whose reason holds a text, whatever its case, then those of a period of days, reads the sentence of a period that starts before the 45 days, over a page of changes and over an empty one, the sentences of a period outside them, in the future and reversed, and that an invalid bound counts as absent, with axe and the 360 px viewport on every page it crosses.
- `e2e/responsive.test.ts` crosses the page with a text and a period set, the day computed from today, so that it does not drift into the sentence of a period that starts before the 45 days.
