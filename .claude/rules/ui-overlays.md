# Dialogs, sheets, filter sheets and toasts

What the copied Dialog, Sheet and Toast carry for a phone and for reduced motion, the filter sheet built on the Sheet, and how a write's result reaches the staff. The theme and the motion classes of `globals.css` are in `.claude/rules/ui-theme.md`, the other shared components in `.claude/rules/ui.md`, and the trials behind this rule in `docs/ui.md`, **Toast** and **Motion**.

## Dialogs and sheets

- Under `sm`, `DialogContent` docks to the bottom of the screen across its whole width (option 2b), its top corners rounded, its bottom padding raised by `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, and its two buttons share one row in two equal columns. From `sm` it stays centered, at the copied `sm:max-w-sm` or the `sm:max-w-110` that the sanctions' confirmation and the blocked words' change of action pass it. A Sheet takes at most 384 px from `sm`, and the whole width under it when it opens from the bottom.
- The docked Dialog has no zoom (`max-sm:data-open:zoom-in-100` and its exit), and its slide up runs only with motion, so that under reduced motion it fades alone, as it does from `sm`. The two confirmation footers, `SanctionConfirmation`'s and `BlockedWordActionChange`'s, are `grid grid-cols-2 gap-2` under `sm` and `sm:flex sm:justify-end` from it (`e2e/responsive.test.ts`, "a dialog docks to the bottom of a 360 px screen, its two buttons sharing one row").
- A Sheet that opens from the bottom takes `max-h-[88svh]`, and what scrolls in it is `SheetBody`, `min-h-0 flex-1 overflow-y-auto px-4` inside the popup, which is already a flex column, between the header and the footer, so that the close button and the footer stay in view on a tall sheet; a caller wraps its fields in it (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #357, for Gilles to review, since #357 asks for « a scrolling body » and the copied Sheet has none).
- Every `SheetFooter` pads its bottom by `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, whatever the side, since the footer of the navigation's menu, opened from the right, sits at the screen's bottom edge as much (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #357, for Gilles to review, since #357 pads the footer of the bottom Sheet alone).
- What sticks to the top or the bottom of the screen adds `env(safe-area-inset-*)` to its padding, and the root layout's `viewport` sets `viewportFit: "cover"` for it (`src/app/layout.tsx`, #355). The docked Dialog, a Sheet's footer and the toasts' viewport pad it, and no test proves it, since Playwright's insets are 0 (inferred). The navigation's are in `CLAUDE.md`, **Fixed to an edge**, until `.claude/rules/staff.md` holds them (not yet, #438).
- The first Sheet to open from the bottom is the audit log's (#359). A Sheet's scroll with a body taller than 88svh is proven by no test, since that body holds five fields (inferred from the classes, not tried).
- A Sheet slides by `2.5rem` through its copied classes and only fades under reduced motion through `motion-reduce:translate-x-0!` or `motion-reduce:translate-y-0!`, important since the copied offset sits behind `data-[side=…]` and `data-starting-style`. `FilterSheetContent` takes both, since the Sheet of `/records` slides in from the right from `sm`, through `sm:` overrides of the bottom Sheet's classes, which are important too (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #360, for Gilles to review). `e2e/audit-log.test.ts`, "the filter sheet fades in and slides up only with motion", lists the properties its transitions run when it opens.
- A dialog fades in and out and zooms from and to 95% through `tw-animate-css`, and only fades under reduced motion: every dialog passes `DIALOG_ONLY_FADES_WHEN_REDUCED` of `dialog-motion.ts`, as the sanctions' confirmation and the blocked words' change of action do, and `e2e/dialog-opening.ts` reads its opacity and scale at the start of its animation for the flows of both. Docked under `sm`, it slides up from the bottom edge instead of zooming, only with motion, which the same file reads as a `lift`: 0 at 1,280 px, positive at 360 px with motion, 0 under reduced motion (`e2e/sanctions.test.ts`, #357). Its fade and its zoom last the 100 ms of the copied `duration-100`, since any dialog animates as the copied components write it and #235 says nothing of its length (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #235, for Gilles to review).

## Filter sheets and chips

- Under `sm`, a form stacks its fields and its main action takes the full width. Under `md`, the filters that are not the search fold behind one outline button that opens a Sheet, and the filters that are set show under the search as chips, each a link to the same address without that filter, so that removing one needs no client logic (`docs/responsive.md`, **Pages**).
- The Sheet holds a GET form of its own, with its choices and, in a hidden field, the search text of the URL, since its portal renders outside the page's form. The page's form keeps its choices, hidden under `md`, so that a search sent from it keeps the filters set. A field of the Sheet joined to the page's form through its `form` attribute would be sent beside the hidden one of the same name, and a repeated parameter counts as absent. A search text typed and not sent is lost when the Sheet's form is sent (Gilles, 2026-11-03). The copied Sheet keeps Base UI's open state, as the navigation's menu does, so it needs no client leaf of the panel's.
- `FilterSheet` composes `FilterSheetTrigger` and `FilterSheetContent` around `FilterSheetForm`; `FilterChips` and they take strings, a form's address and nodes, no type of a page's filters. They moved to `src/ui/` from `src/audit-log/` when the list of accounts needed them, and `FilterSheet` split into a trigger and a content so that the trigger sits in the page's form and the content outside it (`.claude/rules/react-components.md`, on React's events through a portal) (#360; decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #360, for Gilles to review, since #360 names neither). `FilterFold` sets a `FilterSheet` and its `FilterChips` in the fold that shows under `md`, which `AuditLogFilterFold` calls (#392).
- A `side` that changes at `sm`, which #360 asks for, needs responsive classes on the Sheet's content, since a static `side` prop cannot change with the screen: `FilterSheetContent` takes `rightFromSm` for it, which `FilterSheet` does not pass, since the audit log's opens from the bottom at every width under `md` while the list of accounts uses the pieces apart (`.claude/rules/account-list.md`, **The form**).

## Toasts and failure messages

- A write that lands says so in a toast rather than beside its form (#221, #227): the leaf that holds `useActionState` passes its state to `useSuccessToast`, whose effect adds a toast for each new `{ ok: true }`, with the message as the title and `success` as the type, and none for a failure, nor for a state it has already seen, as when `<Activity>` shows a page again and reruns its effects. A refused write adds no toast, and coming back to a page after a write adds no second one: `e2e/blocked-words.test.ts` and `e2e/sanctions.test.ts` prove both, red with the hook's two guards taken out.
- The toast is added once the result commits, not as the action returns: added from the action, before the commit, it left the first blocked word added without its view transition, since Base UI keeps its toasts in a store read through `useSyncExternalStore`, whose updates React renders synchronously (inferred; `docs/ui.md`, **Toast**). Its commit then waits for the list's view transition to end, so the toast enters right after the words' animations.
- A failure's message stays in the alert region, since a toast leaves after 5 s and that message says what to do next, and « Envoi en cours… » stays in the status region (Gilles, 2026-10-29, on #227).
- `WriteFailureMessage` shows a failure's message in an element of its own inside the alert region, keyed by the message, so that a new message enters again and the same one twice in a row keeps its element and does not (inferred from React's keys): in `destructive`, after a `CircleAlert`, `aria-hidden`, fading in through `tw-animate-css` and sliding down by `0.25rem` only with motion. It sits in `src/ui/` since the sanctions show theirs too (#179).
- The toast is shadcn/ui's Toast on Base UI, rather than Sonner, which `shadcn add sonner` would bring with `next-themes` (#227). `src/app/(staff)/layout.tsx` renders its `Toaster` after the page, so that a toast outlives a move between sections; the login pages have none. Base UI's settings stay: a toast leaves after 5 s and 3 show at most. The viewport is a `region` named « Notifications », in `aria-live="polite"`, which F6 focuses and whose focus or hover pauses the timers; each toast is a `dialog` named by its title, the message, and its « Fermer » is hidden from assistive technologies until the toast is expanded or the button focused. The copied check of the `success` type takes no color, and the close button keeps the CLI's `aria-label`, where every other control without visible text has a French `sr-only` text (#227). A toast bears no `view-transition-name`, so a write's view transition stays the words'.
- Base UI renders the title as an `h2`, which would put a heading in the system's font where the headings take EB Garamond, so the copied `ToastList` renders it as a `<p>`, through `render={<p />}`: the toast then has no description to name its dialog, which 4.1.2 asks of one, and the copied classes draw it in `muted-foreground`, where the captures of #239 draw the message in `foreground` (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #227, for Gilles to review).
- A toast only fades under reduced motion, through four classes the panel gives each toast of the copied `ToastList` (Gilles, 2026-10-29, on #227): `motion-reduce:[transition:opacity_500ms]`, the `data-starting-style` and `data-ending-style` opacity classes, and `motion-reduce:data-ending-style:transform-none!`, important since the copied ending transforms sit behind `:not()`s and data attributes. With motion, a toast's enter and exit run only `transform`, and under reduced motion only `opacity` (`e2e/blocked-words.test.ts`, "a write's toast slides in and out unless motion is reduced, when it only fades"). A toast that leaves from behind another, under reduced motion, jumps under the frontmost one as it fades (inferred from the copied classes).
