# Gotchas of the end-to-end tests

- With `cacheComponents`, the page a navigation leaves stays in the document, hidden with `display: none` inside React's `<Activity>` (`node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/02-guides/preserving-ui-state.md:17`, `:355`): on 2026-10-27, once a click on « Journal d’audit » had reached `/audit-log`, `page.locator("h1, h2")` still found the `h1` and the `h2` of `/blocked-words` (#133). A test that reads every element of a kind keeps the visible ones, through `filter({ visible: true })`.
- A logout that starts before React has hydrated the page's metadata can leave `/login/logged-out` with two identical `<link rel="icon">`, so that a locator of `link[rel="icon"]` resolves to 2 elements (« strict mode violation », 1 run in 300 of the flow of `e2e/favicon.test.ts` as it was, #169). **Read in the code** (Next.js 16.4.2, React DOM `19.3.0-canary-278794d7-20261002`): Next.js wraps the metadata in a `<div hidden>` around `<Suspense name="Next.Metadata">` and says the wrapper « remains empty », since the tags are hoisted out (`node_modules/next/dist/lib/metadata/metadata.js:102-111`); `Head` is keyed by the page (`node_modules/next/dist/client/components/app-router.js:385-387`); deleting a dehydrated boundary has `clearHydrationBoundary` remove the nodes from its start comment to its end comment (`node_modules/next/dist/compiled/react-dom/cjs/react-dom-client.development.js:23996-24047`, called at `:15530-15544`), and deleting a hoistable removes its own `stateNode` (`:15440-15454`); a client-side mount creates its hoistable `<link>` at render (`:12347-12352`), and only a hydrating one looks for a link already in the document (`:15899-15931`). **Observed** (throwaway Playwright probes with a `MutationObserver` and a `history.pushState` hook, the CPU throttled to 4× through CDP `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate`, 4 workers, 2026-10-29): before the click, the page held `<div hidden=""><!--$--><!--/$--></div>` and the server's link in `<head>`, written while the document parsed. Of 60 logouts, 18 held two identical links 700 ms later; in those 18, and in none of the other 42, that link had no React fiber (`__reactFiber$…` key) when `history.pushState` ran. It had none at the click, which follows `load`, in 56 of the 60, and a fiber claimed it between the click and the push in 38 of those 56. None of 60 logouts that first waited for a fiber held two links, and in a second probe 15 of 40 sessions kept the pair through four moves between sections, 500 ms apart. **Inferred**: the pair comes from navigating while the boundary is still dehydrated, so that the deletion clears nothing of the server's link, which no fiber owns, and the next page's link is created beside it; it then lasts as long as the document, since only a fiber removes a link (observed through four moves, the rest from the code); and it costs a staff member nothing, both links naming the same file (Chromium shows one icon, not checked). So the flow waits until `distinctIconHrefs()` holds one href among all the page's icon links, which lets two identical links through and fails two different ones (#169). Under the same throttle, the flow before #169 failed 26 runs of 60 and its fix none of 100 (#169). That wait alone took the staff page's link for the logged-out page's. That link stays in `<head>` for 5 to 11 ms after `history.pushState` (observed with no throttle, in 10 logouts of 10, by a throwaway probe of 2026-10-29, #169, which #263's body quotes) and, in the pair above, for the life of the document. So the flow also sets `data-from-staff-page` on every icon link of the staff page before the logout, and after it waits for a link without that attribute and reads the first one, which the render of `/login/logged-out` creates, since a client-side mount creates its hoistable `<link>` at render (`:12347-12352`); the flow fails when that page renders none (#263). **Observed** (throwaway builds and tests, 2026-10-30, #263): on a build whose `/login/logged-out` had `icons: { icon: [] }` in its `metadata`, so that its built HTML held no `<link rel="icon">` while `/login/failed`'s still did, the flow of #169 failed 10 runs of 10, its wait reading an empty list of hrefs; with a copy of the staff page's link appended to `<head>` before the logout by `cloneNode`, which no fiber owns, that flow passed 10 of 10, and the marking flow failed 10 of 10 with the copy as without it, and 20 of 20 under the 4× throttle with 4 workers, each time on the wait for an unmarked link. On the clean build, the marking flow passed 300 of 300 (`pnpm test:e2e e2e/favicon.test.ts --repeat-each=300`) and 60 of 60 under the throttle with 4 workers. **Inferred**: the appended copy stands for the server's link that stays, both being links no fiber owns; the flow of #169 failed on that build because the head had swapped before its wait ran, and it passes on a logged-out page with no icon link of its own only when the staff page's link stays or its wait lands in the 5 to 11 ms, a narrower gap than #263 described; and only the render of `/login/logged-out` creates an unmarked link, although a hydrating hoistable that finds no matching link also appends one (`:15932-15934`), which would be unmarked if it came after the marking, and none of the 40 runs of the marking flow on that build found one. In the other direction, an update of Next.js or React that kept one icon link across the navigation would leave `ownIcons` empty and fail the flow on a page that is right. The tolerance goes once a Next.js or React update adopts or removes the server's icon link on a client mount: the signal is a copy of the flow, run under the same throttle, that finds one icon link in all after the logout and none still marked, since the unmarked link it reads is there whether the server's link stays or not; `servedIcon()` can then read a locator that must match one element.
- After a full page load, such as the one the panel's link to `/` starts, a test waits for the page's `load` before it submits a form. Otherwise Playwright can click while the page still streams: the browser then posts the Server Action's form natively, with no `Next-Action` header, and the document it leaves throws React's error #412, `Connection closed.` (`node_modules/next/dist/compiled/react-server-dom-turbopack/cjs/react-server-dom-turbopack-client.browser.development.js:4917`), or #419. A `<Suspense>` under a `<ViewTransition>` widens that window, since React's streaming runtime reveals it through `document.startViewTransition` (`node_modules/next/dist/compiled/react-dom/cjs/react-dom-server.node.production.js`): before the header flow waited, logging out failed it in 26 of 60 runs on 6c526f6 and 41 of 60 with the crossfade, and it then passed 179 of 180 (`--repeat-each`, 2026-10-27, #134).
- While `holdDiscord` holds a page's calls to Discord, the page keeps streaming, so a test waits for no `load`, and the skeleton can show before the page's stylesheets apply (inferred): with motion, the skeleton test once found no pulse right after its skeleton showed (1 run of 100, `--repeat-each 25`, 2026-10-28), so it first waits for every `link[rel="stylesheet"]` to have its `sheet`, then passed 160 runs of 160 (`--repeat-each 40`, 2026-10-28).
- The page has no `<title>` for about 0.3 s after some writes, and axe's `document-title` reads `document.title` (`node_modules/.pnpm/axe-core@4.14.0/node_modules/axe-core/axe.js:28513-28516`). Next.js 16.4.2 keys the `Metadata` element of the head of each dynamic response, a Server Action's `refresh()` included, with that request's ID, `requestId + 'm'`, which is new for each request (`node_modules/next/dist/server/app-render/app-render.js:320-331`, `:197-198`, `:1550-1564`), so React unmounts the old `<title>` and mounts a new one, inside a `<Suspense name="Next.Metadata">` that is new too (`node_modules/next/dist/lib/metadata/metadata.js:97-112`): when its metadata is not decoded yet, the boundary commits empty (inferred from the gaps below, not read in the Flight client). A throwaway Playwright probe of the blocked words' writes, 10 writes a run, logging every mutation of `<title>` and every view transition (2026-10-30), saw a new `<title>` element at every write, and 5 gaps with no `<title>` and `document.title === ""` in 150 runs, 1,500 writes, so about 0.3% of them, of 297 to 316 ms. In the 4 gaps logged with their view transitions, the update callback of the write's view transition left no `<title>`, no font or image was loading, and the next commit that mounted it came 21 to 37 ms after that view transition had finished. That React holds that commit behind the running view transition is inferred, not read in React's code. The same remount drops `<meta name="viewport">`, whose element is keyed `requestId + 'v'` (`app-render.js:327-328`), for the same moment (inferred, the probe did not read it). The blocked words' flow waits for that view transition and for the toast (#138, #227), which hides most of the gap and not all of it (inferred), so 200 passes of the flow prove little: `checkAccessibility` waits for the page to have a title before each axe run, which `e2e/check-accessibility.test.ts` proves by taking the `<title>` away for 1 s, where 3 runs of 3 failed on `document-title` without the wait (2026-10-30), and the panel lives with the gap, which only a patch of Next.js could close (decided by the agent on 2026-10-30, on #161, for Gilles to review). The line goes if Next.js stops keying the head's elements with the request's ID.
- Chromium hands assistive technologies the text of an element under `text-transform: uppercase` in capitals, while Playwright's `getByRole` names an element from the DOM's text, as written: on 2026-10-29, `Accessibility.getFullAXTree` over CDP named the search's list « 1 COMPTE COMMENCE PAR « LY » » and the sidebar's groups « ADMINISTRATION » and « MODÉRATION », for the search « ly », where `e2e/records.test.ts` found « … par « ly » » (throwaway Playwright test, #232); #324 replaced that count with the ranks, « 1–3 sur 3 », which the same test now finds and whose capitals were not read back (inferred). So an end-to-end name proves the DOM's text, not what a screen reader receives, and an overline reaches one in capitals, which a screen reader may spell out letter by letter for a short word (inferred, not checked with a screen reader).
- At `load`, a page of the panel is not complete: React still holds the content of each `<Suspense>` in hidden segments, `[id^="S:"]` and `template[id^="B:"]`, that a script reveals later through a view transition, and a control in them measures 0 by 0. A probe of `/blocked-words` at 360 px found 11 segments at `load` and none once the reveal ended (2026-11-03, #355), and the first draft of `e2e/responsive.test.ts`, which measured at `load`, passed on empty boxes. `settle`, in `e2e/settle.ts` since #356, waits for the segments to go and for `document.activeViewTransition` to be `null`; `checkAccessibility` waits for the animations to finish, which does not tell a test when the content is there. The line goes if Next.js or React stops holding a streamed boundary's content in hidden segments.
- While a modal Sheet is open, the page behind it is hidden from the accessibility tree, so a `getByRole` locator of a link behind it finds nothing, and `isVisible()` on it answers `false`: `logOutWithScriptsHeld`, which reads that every link is still visible when the first script is released, was red on the audit log's flow that logs out from the bar's menu, until the links it was given were found by `main a[href]` and `main button[aria-haspopup="dialog"]` (`e2e/audit-log.test.ts`, 2026-11-03, #359). A flow that logs out from the menu reads its links in view with role locators first, then hands the helper locators by CSS. The line goes if Base UI stops hiding the page behind a modal from the accessibility tree, or once `logOutWithScriptsHeld` finds its links itself.
- An `sr-only` element is visible to Playwright: its box is 1 px by 1 px, which `toBeVisible` accepts, so a test that proves a text is hidden below a width reads the box's width, as the navigation's flow does for the group titles of the rail (`e2e/responsive.test.ts`, 2026-11-03, #356).
