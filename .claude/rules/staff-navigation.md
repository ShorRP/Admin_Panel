# The navigation

The panel's navigation, which `src/app/(staff)/layout.tsx` renders through `StatefulStaffHeader`: a sidebar from `lg`, a rail of 64 px from `md` to `lg`, a bar and its menu below `md` (#224, #356). `src/staff/staff-header.test.tsx` and `e2e/staff-header.test.ts` name each case. The rail's classes are in `docs/responsive.md`, **Navigation**; the sources, trials and measures behind a line are in `docs/staff.md`: read it before changing how the links prefetch or the safe areas. The theme toggle and the logo are in `.claude/rules/staff-theme-toggle.md`.

## What it holds

- Every page that the staff check guards has the navigation, in a layout below the root one, so that the error pages show none (`.claude/rules/nextjs.md`, **Error pages**, #61).
- The current section carries `aria-current="page"`, on its own path and on every path below it, such as a record under `/records`.
- The sections sit in groups, each under an overline, a `<p>` rather than a heading, so that the pages' `h1` and `h2` stay their only headings (`.claude/rules/ui-theme.md`, **Fonts**). A list names itself through `aria-labelledby`, by an `id` that names the sidebar or the Sheet, since both render the groups, so that `StaffHeader` still renders from its props alone, where a `useId` would not (#224).
- Every staff member sees « Journal d’audit » and « Tickets de support » (#385); « Journal du jeu » and « Journal du Bot » only one who views that log's channel on Discord (`.claude/rules/game-log.md`, **The link in the navigation**; #394, #396); « Modération » only one who holds a role of `MODERATION_ROLE_IDS`, title included (ADR 0005, #176), where the mockup shows it to all and names its link « Fiches des comptes » (#221, #224).
- « Support » shows together with « Modération », once the member's roles are read, so that it does not move down when « Modération » arrives (decided by the agent on 2026-11-05, on #385, for Gilles to review).
- « Panel du staff » is the brand's label, as #221 and #224 set it, not a name of the panel, which keeps the glossary's (**Admin Panel**, `GLOSSARY.md`) in prose and code (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #224, for Gilles to review).
- « Shor » is written so in the source and set in capitals by `uppercase`, as an overline is, where #224 writes « SHOR » (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #224, for Gilles to review).
- Tab titles follow `%s – Panel de Shor`, the default staying « Panel d’administration de Shor », through the root layout's `metadata.title.template`.
- `/` redirects to `/blocked-words` with a 307, not a permanent redirect, so that a home page can come back, through `redirects()` of `next.config.ts`, which runs before `proxy.ts`. The panel's link is a plain `<a>`, since a `next/link`'s `fetch` of `/` would follow that redirect (`.claude/rules/next-link-prefetch.md`).

## The sidebar and the rail

- The sidebar is an `<aside>`, so a `complementary` landmark, that sticks to the screen's left edge, 252 px wide, a left safe-area inset more (#356), on `sidebar`, as the mockup draws it. The panel's link reads « Shor Panel du staff », and « Confidentialité » is `PrivacyPolicyLink`'s link to the privacy policy, which does not prefetch (#186).
- « Se déconnecter » is a `ghost` Button: an icon button, `size="icon"`, whose text is `sr-only`, in the sidebar's foot, and with its text visible in the Sheet (#221, #224).
- The current section takes `sidebar-accent` as its fill and its icon in `primary`, which holds an icon's 3:1 and not text's 4.5:1, so its text stays `sidebar-accent-foreground` (`docs/ui.md`, **Contrast ratios**, #224); its 2 px bar is in a `<ViewTransition>` named `current-section-bar`, which slides to the next current one (`.claude/rules/ui-theme.md`, **Motion**).
- It is built from plain elements rather than shadcn/ui's Sidebar, a client component with logic of its own, which would reopen **No DOM** and **Framework** of `CLAUDE.md` (Gilles, 2026-10-29, on #224; `docs/staff.md`, **The navigation**).
- From `md` to `lg`, that same `<aside>` is a rail of 64 px, through `max-lg:` classes on the same markup and not a second render, so that the sections, the badge and the member are in the DOM once, under the same accessible names in the three forms (`e2e/responsive.test.ts`, #356; one markup (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #356, for Gilles to review, where `docs/responsive.md` already has it)). The rail's classes, `RailOrnament`, the `title` of each link and the scroll of a short screen are in `docs/responsive.md`, **Navigation**.
- In the rail, the member's name goes `sr-only`, in place of `hidden` as in the bar, so that its link is named by the account's name alone, from its own text, and never by the `title` of `StaffMemberIdentity`, « aldis · Staff · Modération », which describes nothing the link's name needs (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #356, for Gilles to review).
- The line under the name goes `sr-only` there too, in place of `hidden`, so that a screen reader still reads it, and `staff-role-label.ts` gives that line and the `title` the same label (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #356, for Gilles to review).
- The badge of « Mots interdits » is `BlockedWordCountBadge` of `src/blocked-words/` (`.claude/rules/blocked-words.md`, **Navigation**). `StaffHeader` takes it as one node, `blockedWordCount`, as it takes `member`, so that the navigation knows nothing of the blocked words, and the layout fills it under a `<Suspense>` of its own with no fallback, so that the badge delays nothing and shows once Discord answers (#226), where a promise read through `use` in `StaffHeader` had the navigation import `src/blocked-words/` (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #226, for Gilles to review, after the code review of #226).
- The pages sit beside it in the layout's column, which holds the mockup's margin (#224), each side raised to the screen's safe-area inset when that is wider, the left one under `md` alone, since the rail and the sidebar take it from `md` and are as wide as that inset more (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #356, for Gilles to review, since #356 names the bar and the column, and a rail or a sidebar drawn under a notch would hide its controls).
- The column lost its own `p-4` when the navigation came, so that a page's `<main>` carries none (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #224, for Gilles to review).

## The bar and its menu

- Under `md`, the bar, `3.5rem` high plus the screen's top safe-area inset, which it pads, sticks to the top of the page, `sticky top-0 z-40`, so that the menu stays in reach on a long page (Gilles, 2026-11-03, on #354, #356). Its sides are `max(1rem, …)` of the left and right insets.
- The document takes a `scroll-padding-top` of the same height, `html:has([data-slot="bar"])` in `src/ui/globals.css`, so that a link focused just under the bar scrolls in below it, as WCAG 2.4.11, Focus Not Obscured (Minimum), asks: `e2e/responsive.test.ts` reads that it equals the bar's height, which `staff-header.tsx` and `globals.css` both write.
- The menu button, named « Ouvrir le menu » in `sr-only`, opens shadcn/ui's Sheet from the right, the button's side, rather than from the left as #129 planned (Gilles, 2026-10-27, on #137). The Sheet holds the same groups, the current section marked the same way, its bar included though it slides in the sidebar alone, then the theme toggle, the member, « Se déconnecter » with its text visible (#137, #224) and « Confidentialité », a `SheetClose` that renders `PrivacyPolicyLink`, so that a click closes the Sheet (#186).
- The groups and the footer sit in the Sheet's `SheetBody`, under its header, which scrolls on a screen too short for them, a phone in landscape say, the footer's `mt-auto` keeping it at the bottom otherwise, so that the close button stays in view (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #356, for Gilles to review, since the footer inside the body keeps everything scrolling together under the header).
- The switch between the forms happens in CSS, through `md:` and `lg:` variants, never `useIsMobile` (`docs/staff.md`, **The navigation**). A new section joins the sidebar, the rail and the Sheet, never a bar of its own: tabs fixed to the bottom were set aside, since the number of sections to come is unknown (Gilles, 2026-11-03, on #354).

## The member's identity

- `StaffHeader` takes the avatar and the name as one node, `member`, which the layout fills with `StaffMemberIdentity` under `<Suspense>`, and renders it three times, in the sidebar's foot, in the bar and in the Sheet's footer. The avatar comes from the member `requireStaffMember` reads: `StaffMember` carries its `avatarUrl` (ADR 0005), `null` for a member without one (`.claude/rules/ui.md`, **Avatars and skeletons**).
- In the bar, `**:data-[slot=staff-member-name]:hidden` hides the name, so that the avatar alone stays beside the menu button, and the bar's link keeps the name in an `sr-only` text before it, so that all three links are named by the account's name alone (#320).
- For a staff member who moderates, each of the three is one link to their own record, which `StaffHeader` makes from `ownRecord`, a promise of `{ href, name }` or `null` that `ownRecordOnceRead`, in `src/records/own-record.ts`, gives from the same read as `opensModerationOnceRead`: `null` for a staff member who does not moderate and when the read fails. `StaffHeader` reads it through `use`, under a `<Suspense>` whose fallback is the node itself, so that the identity shows, unlinked, until Discord answers, and marks the link `aria-current="page"` on that record alone (#320).
- The type of that promise's value is `StaffHeader`'s own, so that `src/staff/` imports no domain folder, which the cycle records → sanctions → staff → records would break (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #320, for Gilles to review).
- The sidebar's and the bar's links are `AccountLink`s, and the Sheet's is a `SheetClose` that renders a `next/link`, each with `prefetch={false}`. `StaffHeader` makes the link, and not `StaffMemberIdentity`, because the one node renders three times and Base UI's `DialogClose` throws outside a `Dialog.Root`, so that a node that carried it could not stand in the sidebar or the bar (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #320, for Gilles to review).
- The identity and `ownRecordOnceRead` take their href from `recordHrefForModerator`, as the audit log's account `TextLink` does (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #320, for Gilles to review; `.claude/rules/records.md`).
- The line under the name comes from the read whose promise `opensModerationOnceRead` gives `StaffHeader`, so that it costs no call to Discord, where #224 names the promise itself (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #224, for Gilles to review).
- Since #320 that line is drawn by `StaffHeader` under the identity in the sidebar and in the Sheet, under a `<Suspense>` of its own whose fallback is a bar, so that it sits outside the identity's link and the link is named by the account's name alone, which story 32 of #317 asks (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #320, for Gilles to review).

## The layout's reads and client leaves

- The layout reads the member once, through one `requireStaffMember` call whose promise it shares: it fills `member` from it, and hands `StaffHeader`, through `opensModerationOnceRead`, a promise of whether they open « Modération », false when the read fails, since the member's node then redirects or throws. `StaffHeader` reads it through `use`, under a `<Suspense>` of its own with no fallback, so that the other sections show at once and « Modération » and « Support » once Discord answers (#176, #224, #385).
- A page loaded in full reads the member twice, once for the layout and once for the page, where ADR 0005 counts one, and a navigation between sections once; both reads stay, with no request-scoped `cache()` to share them, until Discord answers `getMember` with a 429 (Gilles, 2026-10-27, on #71).
- `StatefulStaffHeader` is a client leaf that reads `usePathname`, since a layout receives no path and stays mounted across the navigations between sections. `StaffHeader` otherwise renders from its props alone, the Sheet keeping Base UI's own open state, but for the theme toggle's leaf: those two are the navigation's client leaves of the panel's own, beside the copied Sheet's (#224, #225). Its test renders it with `react-dom/static`'s `prerender`, given a `progressiveChunkSize` without limit, since past its default the render moves a completed `<Suspense>` behind a `<template>` that a script would fill (`docs/staff.md`, **The navigation**; #176, #224).

## Fallbacks, motion, prefetch and safe areas

- The skeleton is `StaffMemberIdentitySkeleton` (#224), with the shorter bar for the line under the name drawn by `StaffHeader` (#320); the three places hold the name in a `<div>` rather than a `<p>`, since the Skeleton is a `<div>` (#140). The member replaces it with no animation (`.claude/rules/ui.md`, **Avatars and skeletons**). « Modération » and « Support » have no skeleton: nothing shows until Discord answers, since most staff members never see the first and the second waits with it (#176, #224, #385).
- The current section's bar moves through its `transform` alone, since every bar has the same size (`e2e/staff-header.test.ts`, #224). The Sheet only fades under reduced motion, through `motion-reduce:translate-x-0!`, important since the copied offset sits behind `data-[side=right]` and `data-starting-style` (`e2e/staff-header.test.ts`, #137).
- Each section's `next/link`, in the sidebar and in the Sheet, takes `prefetch={false}`, since its prefetch could reach `/login` right after a logout, which `proxy.ts` sends a request without a session to (Gilles, 2026-10-28, on #153; `.claude/rules/next-link-prefetch.md`, `docs/staff.md`, **Prefetch after a logout**). A click on a section then fetches its page, a round trip before its skeleton shows (inferred).
- What sticks to the top or the bottom of the screen adds `env(safe-area-inset-*)` to its padding (`.claude/rules/ui-overlays.md`, **Dialogs and sheets**). #356 pads the bar at the top, left and right, the rail and the sidebar at the left, by their width, and at the bottom, by their foot, and the page's column at its sides (`docs/responsive.md`, **Known limits**; decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #356, for Gilles to review, for the rail and the sidebar, whose paddings #356 leaves open).
