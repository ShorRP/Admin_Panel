# Records

The record of one account at `/records/{userId}` (#176), for the staff members who hold a role of `MODERATION_ROLE_IDS` (ADR 0005): its names, dates, roles and standing on the Discord server, read from Discord at each view, then its sanctions and history (`.claude/rules/sanctions.md`) and the support tickets the account opened (`.claude/rules/support-tickets.md`, **A record's ticket archives**). The list that leads to it is in `.claude/rules/account-list.md`. The tests of `src/records/` and `e2e/records.test.ts` name each case. What a view costs in calls to Discord, where the page departs from the mockup, the measures and the trials behind some choices are in `docs/records.md`: read it before adding a read to a record.

## Reading

- `readRecord` reads the member, else the ban, else the user, and gives no record for an ID that names no account. A member's roles come from `listRoles`, in the order of `rolesByHierarchy` (`.claude/rules/account-list.md`, **Badges**).
- A banned account's author comes from the audit log, since `getBan` returns a reason and a user and no author: `readRecord` reads the `MEMBER_BAN_ADD` entries until the first that targets the account, which is the ban in force (inferred), and makes one `getApplicationUser` for `attributionOf` only then (#285). The ban reads as given through the panel, with « Banni par » and its reason without the panel's prefix, only if that entry counts as the panel's; a ban with no entry, or whose entry is not the panel's, a ban given by hand with a reason in the panel's format included, reads as given in Discord, with its whole reason and no « Banni par » (decided by the agent on 2026-10-30, on #285, for Gilles to review).
- That read comes before the history's, which reads the same entries and `getApplicationUser` again, since a banned account's options skip `readSanctionRefusals` (`src/sanctions/sanction-options.ts:38-39`), with no request-scoped `cache()` to share them, as for `getMember`, until Discord answers with a 429 (decided by the agent on 2026-10-30, on #285, for Gilles to review; `docs/records.md`, **Calls per view**).
- A read that fails is thrown and `error.tsx` replaces the whole record, its sanctions with it (`CLAUDE.md`, **Read failures**); the ticket archives have their own failures (`.claude/rules/support-tickets.md`, **Read failures**).

## Avatars and the link to a record

- Every avatar of an account links to that account's record for a staff member who moderates, and to nothing for one who does not, in every section and every feature to come, but on that record itself: the link holds the avatar and the name beside it, since `MemberAvatar` is `aria-hidden`, and takes `prefetch={false}`. The list's rows do, so do the audit log's rows, for the member who made the change, and so does the navigation's identity (Gilles, 2026-10-30, while grilling the account list and the automatic sanctions; #320).
- `recordHrefForModerator`, in `record-href.ts`, is the one place of the rule: it gives `/records/{id}` to a staff member who moderates and an account with an ID, and `null` to anyone else, so that an author with no ID, such as the « AutoMod » of an automatic timeout, gets no link (#327). The list's rows take their path from `recordHref` beside it, as they are shown to those who moderate alone, and `ownRecordOnceRead`, in `own-record.ts`, takes the navigation's own-record link from it (`CLAUDE.md`, **Navigation**).
- A record shows an account's avatar on the Discord server, else its Discord avatar; an account that is not a member, banned or absent, shows its Discord avatar, which `userAvatarUrl` builds (#176).
- A record's avatar is asked at `?size=128` where the others are asked at `?size=64`: `memberAvatarUrl` and `userAvatarUrl` take an `AvatarSize`, `64` or `128`, as their last parameter, `64` when it is left out so that no other caller changes, and `readRecord` passes its `RECORD_AVATAR_SIZE`, 128, for a member, a banned account and an absent one (the union of two sizes and the default decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #246, for Gilles to review). 128 leaves the 68 px avatar enlarged by 1.06 on a screen of twice the density, where 64 gave 2.13 (inferred; #246 asks for 128, and the size a record's avatar is drawn at is out of its scope; `docs/records.md`, **Measures**).
- `src/records/read-record.test.ts` proves that URL, the only proof of a record's avatar, since the flows of `e2e/records.test.ts` pose none (`docs/records.md`, **Trials**; `CLAUDE.md`, **Avatars**).

## Skeleton

- `RecordDetailsSkeleton` takes the shape of the record: the header, a circle for the avatar, 68 px and 56 px under `sm`, a bar for the name, a pill for the standing badge and a bar for the `@username`, over the rule, then, in `RecordColumns`, the card « Détails », the history's heading over two entries of its timeline and the tickets' heading over a card of two rows (#177, #197, #233). It fills the two columns where both fit, with nothing in the right one, since it cannot know whether the record will offer a sanction, and one column below; « Sanctions », which only the record of a member shows, or, to a staff member who bans, that of an account absent or banned, has none (#176, #179, #181, #227).
