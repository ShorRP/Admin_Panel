# Blocked links

What `/blocked-links` reads and writes: the link rule, the one AutoMod rule that blocks a link, read back from Discord at each view and switched on and off as a whole, with its allowed domains and its exempt roles added and removed (ADR 0011, #479, #481, #482, #483). The tests of `src/link-rule/`, `e2e/blocked-links.test.ts` and `e2e/blocked-links-layout.test.ts` name each case. The page's layout at each width is in `docs/responsive.md`, **`/blocked-links`**; the trials behind the rule's shape are in ADR 0011.

## Names

- The section is « Liens », its URL `/blocked-links` and this rule's name come from the ticket, and its code sits in `src/link-rule/`, the folder of the glossary's **Link rule**, since `CLAUDE.md` names a section's folder after a term of `GLOSSARY.md` and « blocked link » is none (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #481, for Gilles to review).
- Every staff member opens it and switches the rule: `requireStaffMember` and no role list (#481).

## The rule

- `linkRuleOf` finds the rule by its exact name, « Liens bloqués du panel », among the keyword rules; one that differs by a letter, a case or a trailing space is not the panel's, and « Activer » creates the panel's beside it (ADR 0002, #481). Two rules of that name read as the first.
- `newLinkRule` holds what ADR 0011 decides: the two regexes, the four `allow_list` entries of each of the eight default domains (`allowed-domains.ts`), the staff role exempted, the action « Bloquer seulement » with its notice. It sends no `keyword_filter`. A new setting of the rule changes that function and its test (`switch-link-rule.test.ts`).
- `allowedDomainsOf` reads a domain when the four entries are all in the list, and leaves every other entry to « Autres exceptions », in the rule's order, so that an entry added in Discord shows and stays through every write (ADR 0011).
- `linkRuleActionOf` reads the action from the rule's actions with `SEND_ALERT_MESSAGE` left out, so that #486's alert changes nothing of what the page says: « Bloquer seulement », a timeout of 3,600 or 86,400 seconds beside the block, or none, shown as « Réglée dans Discord » (#481).
- `/blocked-words` does not count the link rule, whose name none of its rules' names equals (`beside-blocked-words.test.ts`): its « Autres règles AutoMod » lists it by its name with no keyword, under « Elles se modifient dans Discord », which is not true of this one (a known limit, #481).

## Reads

- The page reads the rules once, with `readLinkRule`, through one promise that each section awaits under its own `<Suspense>` with its skeleton, as `/blocked-words` shares its rules'. Roles are read with it, since the staff role is always listed, and channels only when the rule exempts one, so that a failing read of the channels cannot fail a page that needs none (#481).
- A rule that does not exist reads as « absent » with what « Activer » creates: the default domains, the staff role, « Bloquer seulement ». The card says that the rule does not exist and that no link is blocked, so that nothing reads as in force (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #481, for Gilles to review).
- A role or a channel that Discord no longer lists shows as « Rôle supprimé » and « Salon introuvable »: `listChannels` leaves out the channels the application cannot view (`.claude/rules/discord.md`, **The Discord seam**), and a channel is not told apart from a deleted one (inferred, #481).
- A read failure is thrown, with no catch, and `error.tsx` takes it (`CLAUDE.md`, **Read failures**).

## Writes

- `switchLinkRule` reads the rules, then creates the rule or sends `{ enabled }` and nothing else, so that what the staff changed in Discord, a role, a channel, a domain, an action, stays (`switch-link-rule.test.ts`). The form's `enabled` is the state asked for.
- A switch to the state the rule already has succeeds and writes nothing, as a change of action to the action a word has does (`.claude/rules/blocked-words.md`, **Write results**): two staff members who press together do not fail each other (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #481, for Gilles to review). Cutting a rule that does not exist succeeds too.
- The reason is the panel's: `auditLogReason(staffMember, …)` over « création et activation de la règle des liens », « activation de la règle des liens » or « coupure de la règle des liens » (`link-rule-reason.ts`), so that the audit log attributes it to the staff member (`src/audit-log/audit-log-reason.ts`, ADR 0004).
- With 6 keyword rules, the creation is refused in the panel's own message (`no-room-refusal.ts`), which names the rule and says to delete one in Discord; the 6 and the keyword test are `MAX_KEYWORD_RULES` and `isKeywordRule` of `src/blocked-words/`, imported rather than moved (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #481, for Gilles to review).
- A refusal and Discord unavailable are `{ ok: false, message }`, with the fields the form sent, as typed, when it sent one, `domain` for a domain and none for the switch; anything else is thrown, a missing permission included (#482).
- `addAllowedDomain` and `removeAllowedDomain` re-read the rules and send the rule's `trigger_metadata` with its `allow_list` changed and nothing else (`allowListOf` and `withAllowList` of `link-rule.ts` read and write it, as `keywordsOf` and `withKeywords` do for the blocked words), so that what the staff changed in Discord, « Autres exceptions » included, stays; they take a `StaffMember` and no role (`add-allowed-domain.test.ts`, `remove-allowed-domain.test.ts`, #482).
- `submittedDomain` trims what the staff typed and lowers it, then refuses, in this order, an empty text, a scheme, a path, a query or a fragment, a leading `www.` in any case, a text that is no domain, and a domain longer than `MAX_DOMAIN_LENGTH`. A domain is ASCII labels of letters, digits and hyphens, two at least, ending in letters, so that an IP address and an accented name are refused; its 46 characters are the 60 of an allow list entry less the 14 of `https://www.` and `/*`, the longest of the four entries. Every refusal returns the domain as typed (#482; decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #482, for Gilles to review, for the IP address and the accents, which the ticket does not name).
- The room is counted in entries, as Discord counts it: an add is refused when the `allow_list`'s length plus the entries it would write passes `MAX_ALLOW_LIST_ENTRIES`, 100, the entries of « Autres exceptions » counting, so that 25 domains is the most a rule with no other exception holds. The message gives the count, what the domain asks for, and what frees room, a domain, an entry of « Autres exceptions » or either, according to what the rule holds (#482).
- A domain that is held by half, two or three of its four entries, is no allowed domain: adding it writes the entries it lacks, so that it completes with no duplicate, and removing it writes nothing and leaves them. A domain already read as allowed is refused, the held domains and the typed one compared in lower case, since AutoMod compares the allow list whatever the case (ADR 0011): four entries added in Discord as `Arte.TV` refuse `arte.tv` (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #482, for Gilles to review).
- Removing a domain removes its four entries and them alone, an entry that only looks like one stays; a domain the rule no longer holds is removed with no write, as two staff members who press together do not fail each other (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #482, for Gilles to review).
- Before the rule exists, both writes refuse and say to activate it first: creating it stays « Activer »'s, which would otherwise turn on a rule that nobody asked to turn on (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #482, for Gilles to review).
- The reasons are `ajout du domaine permis « d »` and `retrait du domaine permis « d »` (`link-rule-reason.ts`), in the panel's format, so that the audit log attributes each to the staff member and names the domain (#482).
- `addExemptRole` and `removeExemptRole` re-read the rules and the roles and send `exempt_roles` alone, changed by their own role, so that what the staff changed in Discord stays; they take a `StaffMember` and no role list, and hand `role` back as typed when they refuse (`RoleFields`, #483).
- An add refuses, in this order, no choice, the absent rule, a role Discord no longer lists, @everyone or a role an integration manages (the choice leaves both out, so only a forged form reaches that refusal), a role already exempt, and a 21st role, the staff role counting in the 20 when the rule exempts it, which the message says only then (`MAX_EXEMPT_ROLES`, `fullRolesRefusal`), so that Discord's 400 stays a panel error. `isExemptableRole` is the one test of what the choice offers and of what an add accepts. The fake holds the 20 as Discord documents, and the contract suite cannot try it (`docs/discord.md`, **The fake**).
- A removal refuses no choice, the absent rule and the staff role, whatever the form sends (`STAFF_ROLE_REFUSAL`): no write removes it. A role the rule no longer exempts is removed with no write, and a role Discord no longer lists is removed all the same, named by its ID in the message and the reason (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #483, for Gilles to review).
- The reasons are `ajout du rôle exempté « r »` and `retrait du rôle exempté « r »`, with the role's name when Discord lists it, in the panel's format (`link-rule-reason.ts`, #483).

## Forms

- « Domaines permis » holds two client leaves, `StatefulAddAllowedDomainForm` and `StatefulAllowedDomainList`, each with its own `useActionState`, so that a refused add shows its message under the field, with the domain as typed, and a refused removal under the list; the section is a Server Component that takes both as props (`.claude/rules/components.md`, **Forms**, #482).
- The field has `autoCapitalize="none"` and `spellCheck={false}`, and the hint under it says what the rule blocks even for an allowed domain: a link in `http://`, a subdomain other than `www.`, to add apart, and a link glued to punctuation (ADR 0011).
- A domain stays a chip in `font-mono` with a `ghost` icon cross, named « Retirer « d » » in `sr-only`: 44 by 44 px under `touch:`, 28 by 28 with a mouse. Removing asks for no confirmation, since adding the domain back undoes it, and the chips have no `<ViewTransition>`, so the list draws no `<ul>` while it is empty (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #482, for Gilles to review, since the ticket asks for none). While the rule does not exist, the domains that « Activer » would start with show with no cross, since a removal could only be refused (`removable` of `AllowedDomainList`, false for the status `absent`); the form keeps refusing, with the message that says to activate the rule first.
- `AllowedDomainsSkeleton` draws the card of the form with its field and its button as bars, holding no field, since a fallback form would lose what was typed.
- « Rôles exemptés » holds `StatefulAddExemptRoleForm` and `StatefulExemptRoleList`, each with its own `useActionState`, and its section takes both as props. The choice is a `NativeSelect` named `role`, whose value is the role's ID, with a first choice « Choisir un rôle » and the roles by hierarchy (`rolesByHierarchy`) that the rule does not exempt, `readLinkRule`'s `exemptableRoles`, without @everyone and the roles an integration manages; The staff role has no cross and the section says so (`staffRoleId` of the settings); the other chips, a role Discord no longer lists included, have the same cross as a domain, that of a role Discord no longer lists named « Retirer « Rôle supprimé » (ID) » so that two of them differ for assistive technologies (#483). With no role left to choose, the choice reads « Aucun autre rôle » and is disabled with its button (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #483, for Gilles to review).
- The form is its own `@container`: its choice and its button stack under 28 rem (`@md:`, 448 px) and share a row from it, which the single column reaches from a viewport of 488 px, while the side column, 280 px, stacks them at every width (`ROLE_FIELD_ROW_CLASSES`, `docs/responsive.md`). While the rule does not exist the form stays and refuses, as the domains' does (decided by the agent on 2026-11-17, on #483, for Gilles to review).
- `ExemptRolesSkeleton` draws the title, the form's label, choice and button, a bar for the sentence under them and three chips as bars.

## Where the next sections join

- One component per section, each rendering from its props, one `*FromDiscord` wrapper of the page that awaits `readLinkRule`, one skeleton, and a write of its own in its own module with its own Server Action in `actions.ts`, so that #482 to #486 each add theirs and touch the others no further than `read-link-rule.ts` and the page (#481).
- `WriteResult<Fields>` of `write-result.ts` takes the fields that a failure hands back as typed, `DomainFields` for #482's domain and none for the switch: a section that sends a field of its own types it the same way, and `failure` and `unlessDiscordUnavailable` carry it (`CLAUDE.md`, **Write results**, #482).
- `LinkRuleSettings` is what a section reads. A section that needs something more of the rule, such as #486's alert channel, adds it there, with `linkRuleActionOf` leaving that action out already.

## Traps

- AutoMod spares the owner of the Discord server and the members with Administrator or Manage Server: try a link from an account that holds none of them (ADR 0011, `.claude/rules/blocked-words.md`, **Traps**).
- The fake Discord refuses an `allow_list` of more than 100 entries or an entry of more than 60 characters, and more than 10 `regex_patterns` or one of more than 260 characters, as Discord does: a test that poses a rule with a longer entry is refused, so that a seed keeps its entries to 60 characters (`docs/discord.md`, **The fake**, #482).
- The panel's `/audit-log` lists the link rule's writes under « Liens », each reason as it is while it follows the panel's format (`src/link-rule/link-rule-reason.ts`), and as « Règle modifiée dans Discord » when it does not (`.claude/rules/audit-log.md`, **The link rule's lines**, #505).
