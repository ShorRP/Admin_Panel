# What `src/ui/` shares

The components, hooks and constants that more than one domain uses, the copied shadcn/ui components and how they are copied. The theme, the font, the accessibility level and the motion CSS are in `.claude/rules/ui-theme.md`; the dialogs, sheets, filter sheets and toasts in `.claude/rules/ui-overlays.md`. The measures, the trials and the evidence behind them are in `docs/ui.md`, and the patterns and the checklist that review runs on an interface in `docs/responsive.md`.

## shadcn/ui and copied code

- shadcn/ui, on Base UI, in its `nova` style, with CSS variables, as `components.json` records. A ticket adds the components it is the first to use, through `pnpm exec shadcn add`. `shadcn` is a devDependency, since `shadcn init` makes `globals.css` import `shadcn/tailwind.css` from it and Vercel installs devDependencies for the build. Components take a `className`, their variants use `cva` and `cn` merges classes, and the panel's own follow the same shape.
- After `pnpm exec shadcn add <component>`: read `pnpm-workspace.yaml` (`.claude/rules/dependencies.md`, **Adding a dependency**), and `globals.css` when the component brings theme variables, since the CLI looks for their dark values in a `.dark` rule (`selector:".dark"`, shadcn 4.22.1), which the panel no longer has: they go under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` (inferred from the code, not tried). Then strip the comments and turn the English strings French. `pnpm exec shadcn add <component> --diff` shows how a copied file differs from the registry's. When the CLI offers to overwrite `src/ui/button.tsx`, decline: the registry's hover is `primary/80`.
- Copied code becomes the panel's own. Its English strings turn French, such as the Dialog's two « Close » and the Toast's « Close toast », « Fermer » (#227), and the rest stays as the CLI wrote it, so that `--diff` shows only what the panel changed, but for four kinds of line: those Prettier, the lint and the typecheck change (an `eslint-disable-next-line` for the rules a line trips, a `@ts-expect-error` for the typecheck, `.claude/rules/eslint.md`, **Directives**); the Toast's classes (`.claude/rules/ui-overlays.md`); a class whose colors miss the accessibility level on the panel's theme (the next two bullets); and the classes that adapt a component to a phone and a finger, which it carries beside its own so that no caller repeats them (#355, #357; Gilles, 2026-11-03): the touch sizes, the 16 px of a field under `md`, the `env(safe-area-inset-*)` paddings, the Dialog's docking under `sm` and a Sheet from the bottom's `max-h-[88svh]` with its `SheetBody`, the one component `shadcn add` does not write.
- The default Button's hover is `hover:bg-primary/90` where the CLI wrote `hover:bg-primary/80`, since `primary-foreground` on Hydromel's light `primary` fell below 4.5:1 at 80%, which axe refused end to end (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #222, for Gilles to review).
- The text of a Tab that is not open is `text-muted-foreground` where the CLI wrote `text-foreground/60`, which gave 4.29:1 on the list's `muted` in light and which axe refused on the record's tabs (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #234, for Gilles to review).
- `shadcn init --preset nova` (shadcn 4.22.1, 2026-10-24) also wrote the button to `src/components/ui/`, a `src/lib/utils.ts`, Geist through `next/font/google` in `src/app/layout.tsx`, and `--font-sans`, `--font-heading` and `html { @apply font-sans }` in `globals.css`; #67 undid them. Running `init` again, for a new preset say, would presumably redo them (inferred, not tried).

## What `src/ui/` holds

- `globals.css`, what the CLI copies, and the components more than one domain uses. A component moves here when a second domain needs it, but for one that renders a domain's own type, which stays in its domain for the other to import, since `src/ui/` imports nothing outside itself (`SanctionChangeSummary`, `.claude/rules/sanctions.md`, **Notices and history**). It holds no domain logic, and a component comes in with the first ticket that uses it and leaves with its last user (`docs/ui.md`, **Copied code**).
- `src/ui/` imports itself through `@/ui/*`, never through a relative path, and nothing else through `@/`, since every alias of `components.json` points to `@/ui` and the CLI rewrites imports through them; `admin-panel/ui-imports` enforces it (Gilles, 2026-10-24, on #69).
- `"use client"` appears there only on a copied component the CLI writes it on, which follows no rule the panel can rely on (`docs/ui.md`, **Copied code**). The panel's own components there have none and render from their props alone.

## Shared components

- `Overline`, `OrnamentRule`, `PageHeading` and `PageHeader` set a page's header: the overline, real text in normal case that `uppercase` sets in capitals, the `h1` in the headings' font, 38 px from `sm` and 32 px under it (#357), a sentence, and the rule with a diamond, `aria-hidden`, whose `leadingLine` adds a 24 px line before the diamond (#232, #233, #236). `PageHeader` holds the overline, if any, the `h1` and the sentence of `/records`, `/blocked-words`, `/audit-log` and a record that names no account, where each wrote its classes out before #228 (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #228, for Gilles to review).
- `TextLink`, `TEXT_LINK_CLASSES` and `EmptyListFrame` replace the underlined link and the frame of an empty list that the audit log, the blocked words, `ReportChannelLink` and the list of accounts each wrote for themselves. `TextLink` is a `next/link` to a page of the panel in `foreground`, underlined in `input` and in `primary` on hover, which never prefetches (`.claude/rules/next-link-prefetch.md`) and takes its font size from its caller; `TEXT_LINK_CLASSES` serves a link that is no `next/link`, as `ReportChannelLink`'s, which adds its own `whitespace-nowrap`. `EmptyListFrame` is a `<div>` in a dashed frame bordered in `input`, whose sentence the caller writes as a `<p>` inside it, with only the caller's margin or stack in its `className`; an empty list says so in one sentence in it (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #256, for Gilles to review, since #256 asks for a shared definition and leaves open its form, its element and `prefetch`).
- `AccountLink` draws a `next/link` with `prefetch={false}` to the href it is given, and a `div` with the same classes from `null`, and knows nothing of records or rights (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #320, for Gilles to review).
- `Pagination` and `pageNumbersOf` are the page navigation of the list of accounts and of the support tickets' archives, as `pagination.test.tsx` and `page-numbers.test.ts` state. Each number is a `TextLink` with `sr-only` text « Page » and 24 px at least each way, and a single page that a gap would hide shows in its place (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #322, for Gilles to review). Under `sm`, a second list, `pageNumbersOf(page, pages, { compact: true })`, shows at most 5 numbers, the two arrows are 44 px squares with their text `sr-only`, and an empty `aria-hidden` square keeps the place of an arrow that leads nowhere (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #360, for Gilles to review).
- At 360 px, 5 numbers and 2 arrows at 44 px leave no room for a « … », so its `<li>` is 0 px wide and `pointer-events-none`, which `e2e/account-list.test.ts` reads. Only `compact` stays of the `{ siblings: 1, compact: true }` that #360 and `docs/responsive.md` write, since `siblings` has no other value (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #360, for Gilles to review).
- The audit log's page is built from components that know nothing of it, so that the game log and the Bot log take the same template (#392): `LogPage`, `LogFrame`, `LogDay`, `LogRow`, `LogPagination`, `LogEmpty`, `LogCutNote` with `cutEmptySentence` (#395) and `LogSkeleton`, their classes in `log-layout.ts`; `FilterPills`, `FilterForm` with `FilterFormSkeleton` and `FilterFold`, with `filter-layout.ts` for what the pages and their skeletons share; `ParisDay`, `parisDayOf` and `groupByParisDay`; and, for the logs read from a channel, `DiscordMessageLink`, `LogTitle`, `LogDetail` and `LogRawFields` (#396). `FilterFields`, `PERIOD_FIELDS`, `searchTextField`, `filterChipsOf` and `formatDay` draw a log's text and day inputs, and `filter-params.ts` reads them (`dayParamOf`, `textParamOf`).

## Buttons

- `default` is for what a form is for, such as « Ajouter », « Chercher », « Filtrer » or « Réessayer »; `outline` for what undoes or leaves, such as the lifting of a sanction (`.claude/rules/sanctions.md`, **Buttons**); `ghost` for the icon buttons that undo, such as the cross and the pencil of a blocked word's tile (`.claude/rules/blocked-words.md`, **Buttons**). The buttons of the login pages and of the navigation are in `CLAUDE.md`, **Buttons**, until `.claude/rules/staff.md` holds them (not yet, #438).
- Each button carries a lucide icon before its text, `aria-hidden` and marked `data-icon="inline-start"`, which the copied Button's classes make room for; a pending button's `LoaderCircle` takes its icon's place and spins only under `motion-safe`. Icons come from `lucide-react`, shadcn/ui's default, and the French text stays the button's name.
- The panel uses no `destructive` variant, whose red text on its own tint misses 4.5:1 on hover (`docs/ui.md`, **Contrast ratios**), and it stays as the CLI wrote it; the red buttons are `default` Buttons with classes of their own (`.claude/rules/sanctions.md`, **Buttons**).
- Under `touch:` every Button is 44 px high whatever `h-*` a caller passes unprefixed, since the compiled CSS puts `.touch\:h-11` after `.h-9\.5` at the same specificity (`docs/ui.md`, **Touch targets**): the 38 or 40 px a rule gives a button are the desktop's.

## Avatars and skeletons

- A member shows with their avatar on the Discord server, else their Discord avatar, else their initials on `muted`, through shadcn/ui's Avatar on Base UI, which also shows the initials while the image loads and when it fails; an animated avatar shows still (#129). Images come straight from Discord's CDN, as `.webp` at `?size=64` without `animated=true`, which Discord's docs name only for the animated version (https://docs.discord.com/developers/reference, **Image Formatting**, read 2026-10-27); a record's is asked at `?size=128` (`.claude/rules/records.md`). Base UI's `Avatar.Image` renders the `<img>` once the image has loaded, so the panel writes none and needs no `next/image`, and the Content Security Policy allows `https://cdn.discordapp.com` in `img-src` (`.claude/rules/nextjs.md`, **Headers**).
- `MemberAvatar` draws the URL it is given, or the first letter of at most two words of the name, in capitals, skipping the words that start with neither a letter nor a digit, so that « Lyra | Garde de Blancherive » gives « LG » where it gave « L| » (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #230, for Gilles to review). The name sits beside it, so `MemberAvatar` is `aria-hidden`, initials included, and its image's `alt` is empty (#140).
- A fallback is a skeleton in the shape of what loads, from shadcn/ui's Skeleton, one in each domain folder (#139), each a `role="status"` element whose one `sr-only` text names in French what loads, and whose pulse stops under reduced motion (`motion-reduce:animate-none`). The navigation's own are in `CLAUDE.md`, **Suspense fallbacks**, until `.claude/rules/staff.md` holds them (not yet, #438).
- `SuspenseWithSkeleton` wraps the skeleton in a `<ViewTransition>` whose `exit` is `fade-exit` and the content in one whose `enter` is `content-enter`, both with `default` set to `none`, so that the content enters in place of the skeleton; `e2e/skeletons.test.ts` proves it and fails with those `<ViewTransition>`s or a skeleton's `motion-reduce:animate-none` taken out. The pages use it and a layout does not, since a `<ViewTransition>` in the layout leaves an animation behind at every navigation (`.claude/rules/view-transitions.md`) (Gilles, 2026-10-27, on #139).

## Touch targets, type and fields

- Every control a finger can reach is at least 44 by 44 px: buttons, links that stand alone, fields, selects, tabs, pills, the rows of a list, each radio and checkbox with its label, the close buttons of the Sheet and the toast. `globals.css` defines `@custom-variant touch`, which holds under 1,024 px and under `(pointer: coarse)` (`src/ui/touch-variant.test.ts`), and the copied components carry the touch size beside their own, so that a desktop with a mouse keeps its density while a tablet in landscape, and a desktop window narrower than 1,024 px, get the touch sizes (Gilles, 2026-11-03).
- These 44 px are WCAG 2.5.5's, Target Size (Enhanced), a level AAA criterion beyond the AA of `.claude/rules/ui-theme.md`, **Accessibility**, which the panel takes for the finger alone (Gilles, 2026-11-03). A link inside a sentence is exempt, as 2.5.5 and 2.5.8 exempt it, and the 24 px of 2.5.8 stays the floor everywhere else.
- `TextLink` stands alone by default, so a link inside a sentence passes `inSentence`, and `AccountLink` is 44 px high with or without an address. The Toast's, the Sheet's and the Dialog's close buttons keep their glyph in its corner through offsets of their own. The decisions of #357 that stand behind them are in `docs/ui.md`, **Touch targets**.
- A height that a caller sets still beats `touch:h-11` when its selector is more specific, as the `[&>select]:h-9.5` of the list of accounts' form did, so such a caller adds `[&>select]:touch:h-11`, as `BlockedWordActionSelect` and the six choices of the list of accounts do (`docs/ui.md`, **Touch targets**).
- A field or a native `<select>` under `md` takes `text-base`, 16 px, so that iOS does not zoom on focus (inferred from iOS Safari's usual behavior, not tried on a device): `Input` and `Textarea` already did, and `NativeSelect` takes `text-base md:text-sm`.
- No text is smaller than 11 px at any width: `Overline` is 11 px and no caller sets a smaller size, the navigation's brand label « Panel du staff » included (Gilles, 2026-11-03; `docs/ui.md`, **Type and fields**, for that label's mark).
