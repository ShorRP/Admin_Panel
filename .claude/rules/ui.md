# What `src/ui/` shares

The components, hooks and constants that more than one domain uses, the copied shadcn/ui components and how they are copied. The theme, the font, the accessibility level and the motion CSS are in `.claude/rules/ui-theme.md`; the dialogs, sheets, filter sheets and toasts in `.claude/rules/ui-overlays.md`. The measures, the trials and the evidence behind them are in `docs/ui.md`, and the patterns and the checklist that review runs on an interface in `docs/responsive.md`.

## shadcn/ui and copied code

- shadcn/ui, on Base UI, in its `nova` style, with CSS variables, as `components.json` records, added through `pnpm exec shadcn add`. `shadcn` is a devDependency, since `shadcn init` makes `globals.css` import `shadcn/tailwind.css` from it and Vercel installs devDependencies for the build. Components take a `className`, their variants use `cva` and `cn` merges classes, and the panel's own follow the same shape.
- After `pnpm exec shadcn add <component>`: read `pnpm-workspace.yaml` (`.claude/rules/dependencies.md`, **Adding a dependency**), and `globals.css` when the component brings theme variables, since the CLI looks for their dark values in a `.dark` rule (`selector:".dark"`, shadcn 4.22.1), which the panel no longer has: they go under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` (inferred from the code, not tried). Then strip the comments and turn the English strings French. `pnpm exec shadcn add <component> --diff` shows how a copied file differs from the registry's. When the CLI offers to overwrite `src/ui/button.tsx`, decline: the registry's hover is `primary/80`.
- Copied code becomes the panel's own. Its English strings turn French, such as the Toast's « Close toast », « Fermer », and the rest stays as the CLI wrote it, so that `--diff` shows only what the panel changed, but for four kinds of line: those Prettier, the lint and the typecheck change (`.claude/rules/eslint.md`, **Directives**); the Toast's classes (`.claude/rules/ui-overlays.md`); a class whose colors miss the accessibility level on the panel's theme (the next two bullets); and the classes that adapt a component to a phone and a finger, which it carries beside its own so that no caller repeats them (Gilles, 2026-11-03): the touch sizes, the 16 px of a field under `md`, the `env(safe-area-inset-*)` paddings, the Dialog's docking under `sm` and a Sheet from the bottom's `max-h-[88svh]` with its `SheetBody`, the one component `shadcn add` does not write.
- The default Button's hover is `hover:bg-primary/90` where the CLI wrote `hover:bg-primary/80`, since `primary-foreground` on Hydromel's light `primary` falls below 4.5:1 at 80% (`docs/ui.md`, **Copied code**). It gets no tests of its own, though the theme test proves the palette on that hover's fill (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #222, for Gilles to review, since #222 fixes `primary`).
- The text of a Tab that is not open is `text-muted-foreground` where the CLI wrote `text-foreground/60`, which misses 4.5:1 on the list's `muted` in light (`docs/ui.md`, **Copied code**) (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #234, for Gilles to review).
- Running `shadcn init` again would presumably write again what the panel removed, a button in `src/components/ui/`, a `src/lib/utils.ts`, Geist and font variables in `globals.css`: remove them (`docs/ui.md`, **Copied code**; inferred, not tried).

## What `src/ui/` holds

- `globals.css`, what the CLI copies, and the components more than one domain uses. A component moves here when a second domain needs it, but for one that renders a domain's own type, which stays in its domain for the other to import, since `src/ui/` imports nothing outside itself (`SanctionChangeSummary`, `.claude/rules/sanctions.md`, **Notices and history**). It holds no domain logic, and a component comes in with the first ticket that uses it (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #230, for Gilles to review).
- `src/ui/` imports itself through `@/ui/*`, never through a relative path, and nothing else through `@/`, since every alias of `components.json` points to `@/ui` and the CLI rewrites imports through them; `admin-panel/ui-imports` enforces it (Gilles, 2026-10-24, on #69).
- `"use client"` appears there only on a copied component the CLI writes it on, which follows no rule the panel can rely on (`docs/ui.md`, **Copied code**). The panel's own components there have none and render from their props alone.

## Shared components

- `Overline`, `OrnamentRule`, `PageHeading` and `PageHeader` set a page's header: the overline, real text that `uppercase` sets in capitals, the `h1` in the headings' font, 38 px from `sm` and 32 px under it, a sentence, and the rule with a diamond, `aria-hidden`, whose `leadingLine` adds a 24 px line before the diamond. `PageHeader` holds the overline, if any, the `h1` and the sentence of `/records`, `/blocked-words`, `/audit-log` and a record that names no account (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #228, for Gilles to review).
- `TextLink` is a `next/link` to a page of the panel in `foreground`, underlined in `input` and in `primary` on hover, which never prefetches (`.claude/rules/next-link-prefetch.md`) and takes its font size from its caller; `TEXT_LINK_CLASSES` serves a link that is no `next/link`, as `ReportChannelLink`'s, which adds its own `whitespace-nowrap`. `EmptyListFrame` is a `<div>` in a dashed frame bordered in `input`, whose sentence the caller writes as a `<p>` inside it, with only the caller's margin or stack in its `className`; an empty list says so in one sentence in it (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #256, for Gilles to review, since #256 asks for a shared definition and leaves open its form, its element and `prefetch`).
- `AccountLink` draws a `next/link` with `prefetch={false}` to the href it is given, and a `div` with the same classes from `null`, and knows nothing of records or rights (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #320, for Gilles to review).
- `Pagination` and `pageNumbersOf` are the page navigation of the list of accounts and of the support tickets' archives (`pagination.test.tsx`, `page-numbers.test.ts`): each number is a `TextLink` with `sr-only` text « Page » and 24 px at least each way, and a single page that a gap would hide shows in its place (decided by the agent on 2026-11-02, on #322, for Gilles to review).
- Under `sm`, `pageNumbersOf(page, pages, { compact: true })` shows at most 5 numbers, the two arrows are 44 px squares with their text `sr-only`, an empty `aria-hidden` square keeps the place of an arrow that leads nowhere, and a « … » takes no width, its `<li>` 0 px wide and `pointer-events-none`, so that 5 numbers and 2 arrows fit a 360 px screen (`docs/ui.md`, **Touch targets**); only `compact` stays of the `{ siblings: 1, compact: true }` of `docs/responsive.md`, since `siblings` has no other value (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #360, for Gilles to review).
- `frenchNumber` writes a number as French does, with a narrow no-break space between the thousands.
- `parisDayStart` gives the instant at which a day of Paris, written `yyyy-mm-dd`, starts.
- `parisDayAfter` gives the instant at which the day after it starts, where that day ends.
- The audit log's page is built from components that know nothing of it, so that the game log and the Bot log take the same template: `LogPage`, `LogFrame`, `LogDay`, `LogRow`, `LogPagination`, `LogEmpty`, `LogCutNote` and `LogSkeleton`, with their classes in `log-layout.ts`; `FilterPills`, `FilterForm`, `FilterFold` and `FilterFields`, with `filter-layout.ts` for what the pages and their skeletons share and `filter-params.ts` to read a text and a day; `ParisDay` and `groupByParisDay`; and, for the logs read from a channel, `DiscordMessageLink`, `LogTitle`, `LogDetail` and `LogRawFields`.

## Buttons

- `default` is for what a form is for, such as « Ajouter », « Chercher », « Filtrer » or « Réessayer »; `outline` for what undoes or leaves, such as the lifting of a sanction (`.claude/rules/sanctions.md`, **Buttons**); `ghost` for the icon buttons that undo, such as the cross and the pencil of a blocked word's tile (`.claude/rules/blocked-words.md`, **Buttons**). The buttons of the login pages and of the navigation are in `CLAUDE.md`, **Buttons**.
- Each button carries a lucide icon before its text, `aria-hidden` and marked `data-icon="inline-start"`, which the copied Button's classes make room for; a pending button's `LoaderCircle` takes its icon's place and spins only under `motion-safe`. Icons come from `lucide-react`, shadcn/ui's default, and the French text stays the button's name.
- The panel uses no `destructive` variant, whose red text on its own tint misses 4.5:1 on hover (`docs/ui.md`, **Contrast ratios**), and it stays as the CLI wrote it; the red buttons are `default` Buttons with classes of their own (`.claude/rules/sanctions.md`, **Buttons**).
- Under `touch:` every Button is 44 px high whatever `h-*` a caller passes unprefixed, since the compiled CSS puts `.touch\:h-11` after `.h-9\.5` at the same specificity (`docs/ui.md`, **Touch targets**): the 38 or 40 px a rule gives a button are the desktop's.

## Avatars and skeletons

- A member shows with their avatar on the Discord server, else their Discord avatar, else their initials on `muted`, through shadcn/ui's Avatar on Base UI, which also shows the initials while the image loads and when it fails; an animated avatar shows still. Images come straight from Discord's CDN, as `.webp` at `?size=64` without `animated=true` (`docs/ui.md`, **Avatars**); a record's is asked at `?size=128` (`.claude/rules/records.md`). Base UI's `Avatar.Image` renders the `<img>` once the image has loaded, so the panel writes none and needs no `next/image`, and the Content Security Policy allows `https://cdn.discordapp.com` in `img-src` (`.claude/rules/nextjs.md`, **Headers**).
- `MemberAvatar` is `aria-hidden`, initials included, and its image's `alt` is empty, since the name sits beside it. It draws the URL it is given, or the first letter of at most two words of the name, in capitals, skipping the words that start with neither a letter nor a digit, so that « Lyra | Garde de Blancherive » gives « LG » (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #230, for Gilles to review).
- A fallback is a skeleton in the shape of what loads, from shadcn/ui's Skeleton, one in each domain folder: each but `BlockedWordCapacitySkeleton`, which is `aria-hidden`, is a `role="status"` element whose one `sr-only` text names in French what loads, and its pulse stops under reduced motion (`motion-reduce:animate-none`). The page skeletons are in the rule of their domain (`blocked-words.md`, `audit-log.md`, `account-list.md`, `support-tickets.md`, `records.md`, under `.claude/rules/`), and the navigation's in `CLAUDE.md`, **Suspense fallbacks**.
- `SuspenseWithSkeleton` wraps the skeleton in a `<ViewTransition>` whose `exit` is `fade-exit` and the content in one whose `enter` is `content-enter`, both with `default` set to `none`, so that the content enters in place of the skeleton (`e2e/skeletons.test.ts`). The pages use it and a layout does not, since a `<ViewTransition>` in the layout leaves an animation behind at every navigation (`.claude/rules/view-transitions.md`) (Gilles, 2026-10-27, on #139).

## Touch targets, type and fields

- Every control a finger can reach is at least 44 by 44 px: buttons, links that stand alone, fields, selects, tabs, pills, the rows of a list, each radio and checkbox with its label, the close buttons of the Sheet and the toast. `globals.css` defines `@custom-variant touch`, which holds under 1,024 px and under `(pointer: coarse)` (`src/ui/touch-variant.test.ts`), and the copied components carry the touch size beside their own, so that a desktop with a mouse keeps its density while a tablet in landscape, and a desktop window narrower than 1,024 px, get the touch sizes (Gilles, 2026-11-03).
- These 44 px are WCAG 2.5.5's, Target Size (Enhanced), a level AAA criterion beyond the AA of `.claude/rules/ui-theme.md`, **Accessibility**, which the panel takes for the finger alone (Gilles, 2026-11-03). A link inside a sentence is exempt, as 2.5.5 and 2.5.8 exempt it, and the 24 px of 2.5.8 stays the floor everywhere else.
- `TextLink` stands alone by default, so a link inside a sentence passes `inSentence`: three of its four callers stand alone, and a caller that forgets the prop is left with a larger target, where the opposite default leaves it a small one (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #357, for Gilles to review).
- `AccountLink` is 44 px high with or without an address, so that a row's height does not follow the staff member's rights, and its `touch:flex` puts the content of a caller that is a `block` in the middle of the 44 px (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #357, for Gilles to review).
- The Toast's close button takes `touch:-my-2 touch:-mr-2`, so that a toast keeps its height, and the Sheet's and the Dialog's keep their glyph in its corner through `touch:top-1 touch:right-1` and `touch:top-0 touch:right-0` (decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #357, for Gilles to review).
- A height that a caller sets still beats `touch:h-11` when its selector is more specific, as the `[&>select]:h-9.5` of the list of accounts' form did, so such a caller adds `[&>select]:touch:h-11`, as `BlockedWordActionSelect` and the six choices of the list of accounts do (`docs/ui.md`, **Touch targets**).
- A field or a native `<select>` under `md` takes `text-base`, 16 px, so that iOS does not zoom on focus (inferred from iOS Safari's usual behavior, not tried on a device): `Input` and `Textarea` already did, and `NativeSelect` takes `text-base md:text-sm`.
- No text is smaller than 11 px at any width: `Overline` is 11 px and no caller sets a smaller size, the navigation's brand label « Panel du staff » included, which makes the panel's link 1.5 px taller (`docs/ui.md`, **Type and fields**) (Gilles, 2026-11-03; decided by the agent on 2026-11-03, on #357, for Gilles to review, since #357 names the overlines of `text-[0.625rem]` and not that class).
