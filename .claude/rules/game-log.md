# The game log's page (#394, #395)

`/game-log`, under « Journaux » in the navigation, lists the lines that the game server's webhook posts in « 🔧・log-jeux » (`GLOSSARY.md`, **Game log**; ADR 0010, #381). It is built on the log components of `src/ui/` (`CLAUDE.md`, **`src/ui/`**), as the audit log and the Bot log are, and filtered by category, through pills, and by action, character, game account, target, cell, plugin, reason, text and period (**The filters**, #395). This file holds what a change to `src/game-log/` must not break.

## Configuration and access

- `GAME_LOG_CHANNEL_ID` and `GAME_LOG_WEBHOOK_ID` are the snowflakes of the channel and of the webhook that posts there, never the webhook's URL, a secret the panel does not hold. `parseGameLogEnv`, in `src/game-log/env.ts`, gives `{ gameLog: { channelId, webhookId } }` or `{ gameLog: null }`: both unset or empty count as unset, one without the other is refused by the name of the missing one, and a value that is no snowflake is refused by the name of its variable, never its value (`src/game-log/env.test.ts`). `next.config.ts` parses them at build when `VERCEL` is set, so a half set pair fails the build, and `configuredGameLog()` parses them again at the first request elsewhere. A channel read without its webhook would take any author's line for the game server's, which ADR 0010 forbids, so a half set pair is a misconfiguration rather than a game log left out (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #394, for Gilles to review, since #394 says only that the channel and the webhook's ID enter the configuration).
- The values: « 🔧・log-jeux » `1552824453153423520` and the webhook « Shor » `1552824522292330506` on Shor; « log-jeux » `1568022814483685377` and its test webhook `1568023050346307647` on Gilles' test Discord server (#390, Gilles, 2026-11-05). Setting them in Production and Preview on Vercel is Gilles' step, then a redeploy, since a variable reaches a deployment at its build (`env.md`).
- Only a staff member who views the channel on Discord has the link and the page. The page starts with `requireStaffMember`, then `readGameLogForStaff`, in `src/game-log/read-game-log-for-staff.ts`, which answers `unconfigured` for a game log left out of the configuration, `hidden` unless `canViewChannel` says the staff member views the channel (**Channel visibility** of `CLAUDE.md`), and otherwise the page read; the page only calls `notFound()` for `hidden` (`src/game-log/read-game-log-for-staff.test.ts`). That runs in the part of the page under `<Suspense>`, so that the static `metadata` stays and the not-found page of a staff member who does not view the channel renders beside the navigation, under the tab « Journal du jeu – Panel de Shor », as `/records`' does (`e2e/game-log.test.ts`).
- A game log left out of the configuration shows no link, and its page, which the whole staff reaches by its URL, says « Le journal du jeu n’est pas configuré pour ce serveur Discord. » after the staff check alone, since there is no channel to view (`GameLogNotConfigured`; decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #394, for Gilles to review, since #394 says only « Pour les autres, c'est la page introuvable »).

## The link in the navigation

- The layout hands `StaffHeader` a promise, `viewsGameLog`, from `viewsChannelOnceRead(discord, configuredGameLog(), staffMember)`, in `src/staff/views-channel-once-read.ts`, which the Bot log's link takes too (#396): false for a game log left out of the configuration, without asking Discord, for a staff check that failed, and for a `DiscordError` of the read of the channels; any other error is thrown, so that a bug is not hidden. In `StaffHeader` the game log is an item of the `logs` list, after « Journal d’audit », whose `shown` is that promise: `ListedSectionLink` reads an item's `shown` through `use`, under a `<Suspense>` of its own with no fallback, so that the audit log's link shows at once and the game log's arrives with Discord's answer, and the Bot log of #381 joins the list as one item with a promise of its own (`docs/responsive.md`). The same rule as the blocked words' count (`CLAUDE.md`, **Read failures**): Discord unavailable never replaces a page with `error.tsx` for a link, and a panel error there alerts no one either, the layout's staff check having read the member through the same token first (inferred) (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #394, for Gilles to review).
- The layout reads `getGuild` and `listChannels` at each full load for that link, which `canViewChannel` needs. A probe that counted the fake's calls during the load of `/support-tickets`, where the page reads them too, saw one call of each, not two (a throwaway Playwright test, 2026-11-06): the layout's read and the page's collapse into one request, which is inferred to be Next.js's memoization of identical GET `fetch` calls within one render. A failure injected for that page so reaches the layout too, which gives no link, and `e2e/support-tickets.test.ts` fails `listChannels` once, as it did before the game log. The first version of this change made `FakeDiscord.failNext` take a count for a race that does not exist, and dropped it.

## Reading

- `readGameLog(discord, { channelId, webhookId }, searchParams)`, in `src/game-log/read-game-log.ts`, asks Discord's search for the channel, the webhook as `author_id`, newest first, 25 messages a search, with `min_id` and `max_id` from the period and the cursor, and one term as `content` when a filter can be served (**The filters**). It reads through `scanMessages`, up to 4 searches a view, beside the staff check's `getMember` and the channels' read of `requireChannelAccess`, against the rate limit that ADR 0010 measures (`CLAUDE.md`, **Retries**). It asks no member, no user and no other route.
- It checks `author.id` and `channel_id` again in memory, since ADR 0010 found that a webhook's message carries the webhook's ID as its author's, and a forged line must not pass if the search lets it through (`src/game-log/read-game-log.test.ts`, through a fake that ignores `author_id`).
- The time of a line is its message's date, taken from its ID, never the embed's timestamp: the period and the cursor, which the search serves by message IDs, then agree with the day under which the page groups the line (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #394, for Gilles to review).
- Pages go by cursor, not by offset. `before` holds the ID of the last line of a page and is sent back as `max_id`, through the shared `cursorParamOf` of `src/ui/filter-params.ts`, which the audit log's `before` reads too; « Revenir aux plus récents » drops it, and the page ends on « Jusqu’au <date> » as the audit log's does. So no page meets the search's offset limit of 9,975, and a line posted between two views pushes no line from one page to the next. « Plus anciens » shows when a page holds 25 lines, a full page, or is cut (**The filters**), and not from `total_results`: the contract suite shows `total_results` counting every page of a search with no bound (`src/discord/discord-api-contract.ts`, the test of `limit` and `offset`) and never under a `min_id` or a `max_id`, so that an older page whose total counts only what lies under its `max_id` is not proven (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #394, for Gilles to review, after the review of #394). Two limits follow. A game log of exactly 25 lines, or a last page of 25, leads to an older page that says « Aucune ligne plus ancienne. ». And Discord's index lags and may give a page fewer than 25 lines though older ones exist (ADR 0010; the docs warn that « search may return slightly fewer results than the limit specified »), in which case no « Plus anciens » shows, and a line missing from a page is on no other, since the next starts under the last line shown (inferred). A cursor that is no snowflake counts as absent.
- `max_id` is the nearer of the cursor and the end of the period, and the read asks Discord nothing when the period ends before it starts or when no ID can lie between the two bounds, which spares a search whose answer for a reversed range was not tried: `searchBounds`, in `src/discord/period-bounds.ts`, works both out for this page and the Bot log's (`src/discord/period-bounds.test.ts`, `src/game-log/read-game-log.test.ts`; moved there by #396, which also moved the page size, `SEARCH_PAGE_SIZE`, and the grouping of a page by day, `groupByParisDay` of `src/ui/`).

## The lines

- `parseGameLogMessage`, in `src/game-log/parse-game-log-message.ts`, reads a line in the layout of the script that Anaisenkit gave Gilles (ADR 0010, **What a game log line holds**): the title `<category> · <action>`; a first field named after the character, whose value is `compte <n>` and, on a second line, `Cible : <n>` when the action has a target; a field `Lieu`; the footer `Shor · <hash>`. The script is an example with fixed values, not the game server's code, so the parser reads that layout first and stays tolerant of every other place a piece may sit.
- The character's field is the field whose value starts with `compte <n>` on its first line, found by that value and not by its name or its position, and looked for before the author's name and the lines of the description, so that its `Cible` is read whatever other piece holds a `compte`, which then stays raw under the event (`src/game-log/parse-game-log-message.test.ts`, #412). Its name is the character, `<n>` the game account, each other line of its value goes through the labelled-line reading below, and a line that is none of those stays raw. So a character named `Lieu` is a character, and a line without a target, such as `Personnages · déconnexion`, gives its character and its game account with `target` null. An action is everything after the title's first separator, so `administration:workshop:create` and `déconnexion` stay whole.
- Failing that, the parser looks for the character and the game account in a field's value (`<character> · compte <n>`) or name, then in the author's name, then in the lines of the description (`<character> · compte <n>`, the guess it had before the script), the first found winning; for `Cible`, `Motif` and `Lieu` among the fields' names, the lines of the character's field and the labelled lines of the description (`Label : value`, whatever the markdown around the label and the colon's space); and it takes the identifier from the end of the footer after a `·`. Backticks and bold marks around a value are stripped, and a label with nothing left of its value, such as `Cible : **`, is not read and stays raw. A layout it does not know shows as a line out of format rather than a wrong line (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #412, for Gilles to review, over dropping the earlier readings, since the script is an example).
- The fields of `GameLogEvent` are the names the filters of #395 read: `category`, `action`, `character`, `gameAccount`, `target`, `reason`, `place` and `eventId`, each a string or `null`. `Motif` is read when a line carries one, although the script shows none. The script's message with no embed, whose content is the place then `Shor · <hash> | Hier à 17:23`, stays a raw line, its content as its title, until the game server is known to post one.
- A line is in format when its title has a category and an action; every other piece is optional and shown only if found. What the parser does not recognise is not dropped: it shows raw under the event, in the order Discord shows it, the message's content, an author's name that names no character, each line of the description that is neither the character and the game account nor a `Cible`, `Motif` or `Lieu` (a repeated label included), each field that is none of those, as `name : value`, a footer that holds no identifier, and the text of any other embed, so that no text Discord shows is lost (`GameLogEvent.extra`; `src/game-log/parse-game-log-message.test.ts`, #394). A line that is not in format, an embed with another title or a message with no embed, shows its title, or its content when it has no embed, and each other text as it was written, without failing the page. Either keeps its link to Discord. An embed's timestamp, its images and its colour are not text of the line and are not shown (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #394, for Gilles to review, after the review of #394).
- A line shows, from `GameLog` in `src/game-log/game-log.tsx`: its time in Paris, the tile of its category's icon (`ShieldCheck` for « Administration », `UserRound` for « Personnages », `Gamepad2` for any other, `CircleHelp` for a raw line), `<category> · <action>` with the action in the monospace font, `<character> · compte <n>`, `Cible : …` and `Motif : …` on a line, `Lieu : …`, `Identifiant : …`, and « Voir dans Discord », a plain `<a>` to `https://discord.com/channels/<server>/<channel>/<message>` in a new tab, 44 px high under `touch`. The game account is a number, not a link to a record, until the game links it to a Discord account (`docs/agents/open-questions.md`). Every detail takes `wrap-anywhere`, so that a place, a reason or a name of any length wraps at 360 px (`e2e/responsive.test.ts`, with a reason of 300 characters, a place of 120 and a raw title of 150).
- The icons by category are the agent's, #394 asking for none (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #394, for Gilles to review). No full list of categories exists: « Administration » and « Personnages » are the two known, and Anaisenkit knows no more (ADR 0010), which is why the pills list those two (**The filters**).

## The filters

- `category`, `action`, `character`, `account`, `target`, `cell`, `plugin`, `reason`, `text`, `from` and `to` are read from the address by `gameLogFiltersOf`, with the shared `textParamOf` and `dayParamOf` of `src/ui/filter-params.ts`; an invalid one, empty, blank, repeated, with no word in it where words are searched, or a day that does not exist, counts as absent. `category` is `administration` or `characters`, English slugs since a URL counts as a file name (`GAME_LOG_CATEGORY_KEYS`); `account` is a number of 1 to 20 digits, its leading zeros dropped so that Discord is asked for the number the game server writes; `cell` is a form ID in hex of 1 to 8 digits, with or without `0x`, kept as typed; `action`, `character`, `target` and `plugin` hold 100 characters at most; `reason` and `text` hold 1,024, `MAX_SEARCHED_TEXT_LENGTH` of `src/discord/message-search-limits.ts`, the length of Discord's search (ADR 0010). The address writes them in that order, then `before`, which `gameLogHref` gives the pills, the form, the Sheet, the chips and the pagination alike.
- The pills are « Tout », « Administration » and « Personnages », the two categories seen: ADR 0010 says that the full lists of categories and actions are « not known, and Anaisenkit knows no more ». #395 asks for pills drawn from the list that #391's questionnaire gives, and that list does not exist, so the pills are a table of two entries, `GAME_LOG_CATEGORIES`, where a category the game server posts later is one more entry, with its icon, when someone learns of it. Until then a line of another category shows under « Tout » with the generic icon and is found by its text or its action, and no pill « Autres » gathers the lines that no pill names, since nothing says that any exists (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #395, for Gilles to review). A line is in a category when its title's category equals the pill's label, whatever the case. A pill keeps the other filters and drops the cursor, and « Réinitialiser » shows once any filter is set. The category is the one filter that is no chip and that « Filtres » does not count, since its pill says it, as the audit log's type and the Bot log's family are; the form and the Sheet keep it in a hidden field.
- The fields are « Action », « Personnage », « Compte de jeu », « Cible », « Cellule », « Plugin », « Motif », « Texte », « Du » and « Au », in that order. « Action », « Personnage », « Cible » and « Motif » are texts of whole words, not choices, as the Bot log's action is: every word typed must be among the words of the line's action, character, target or reason, whatever the case and with the accents, so that « heal » finds `administration:heal` and « hea » finds nothing, and an action the game server adds is found with no change here; #395's comment recommends a text rather than a closed list for the action, which the lists unknown to ADR 0010 allow no other way (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #395, for Gilles to review). The game account is the number alone, equal to the line's, with no link to a record. « Texte » finds the words in one text of the message, as the Bot log's does, and a text with no word in it counts as absent, where #394 sent it to Discord and found nothing: the page checks the text in memory now, and a text that matches everything would be a filter that does nothing. The apostrophe is U+2019 alone in `wordsOf`, as in the fake, so a name typed with the ASCII one is not known to join, which the archives' reason shares (**The fake** of `CLAUDE.md`; inferred, not tried).
- The place is two fields, « Cellule » and « Plugin », read out of the line's `Lieu`, `<form ID in hex>:<plugin> · <x>, <y>, <z>` (ADR 0010; `placeOf`, `src/game-log/game-log-place.ts`). #395 says « par cellule ou par plugin »: two fields make the two combine in AND and cannot be mistaken for each other, where one field would have to guess which a text means (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #395, for Gilles to review). The cell equals the line's form ID whatever its case, its `0x` prefix and its leading zeros; the plugin is any part of the plugin's name, whatever the case, since the plugins are the server's load order, which the panel does not hold, so that no closed choice is possible. « Cellule » is #395's word: ADR 0010 infers that the form ID is a worldspace or a cell local to its plugin, so it may name a worldspace. A place that does not follow that format, and a line with no place, match neither field and stay findable by the text.
- A line out of format, an embed with another title or a message with no embed, matches only the text and the period, and any other filter drops it. A line in format whose category no pill names matches the other filters like any other.
- Discord's search serves one text, so the read asks for one term, the first of the game account, the longest term of the target, of the character, of the text, of the reason and of the category's label, and checks every filter again in memory, exactly, over the messages it gives (`keptEntry`). The term is a necessary condition of its filter, so Discord hides no line that passes. The action, the cell and the plugin are never the term: the game server writes `administration:heal` and `3c:Skyrim.esm · …`, and the search's index may keep a word joined by a colon or a dot in one piece, as a standard tokenizer does and as it joins the letters around an apostrophe U+2019 (ADR 0010), in which case « heal » or « Skyrim » is no word of the index and a search for it would hide lines that the page keeps. **Inferred, unproven**: no probe has put a colon or a dot between letters in a message of the test Discord server and searched one side of it. A read of Discord's search on the test Discord server by Gilles, or a contract test that posts a message holding `administration:heal` and searches `heal`, would settle it; if Discord splits there, the three can join the terms. The same doubt holds for a text or a character typed as part of such a word, which the Bot log's text shares, and the game log's text has gone to Discord since #394. An action, a cell or a plugin alone so reads on: up to 4 searches of 25 messages (`MAX_SEARCHES`, in `scanMessages` of `src/discord/scan-messages.ts`, which the Bot log's read uses too), stopping as soon as the page holds 25 lines. When 4 searches leave fewer, the page is cut: it says that the 100 following messages of the channel were read, and « Plus anciens » continues from the last message read (`LogCutNote` and `cutEmptySentence` of `src/ui/log-cut.tsx`, shared with the Bot log). A view so costs one search when Discord's answer is short or all of it passes, and up to 4 when the filters drop lines, against the rate limit of about one call every 4 to 5 s that ADR 0010 measures (`CLAUDE.md`, **Retries**; not measured on this page).
- From `md` the form sits inline under the pills, its ten fields in lines of `basis-44`, three to a row at 768 and 1,024 px; under `md` it folds behind « Filtres », a button as wide as the column that opens a Sheet from the bottom, with the ten fields stacked, and the filters set show under it as chips of 44 px, each a link to the address without that filter and without a cursor (`FilterFold`). The pills stay in view at every width, 44 px high under `touch`. The skeleton draws three pill bars, the ten fields' bars and five rows.
- A period runs from the first midnight of its first day of Paris to the last midnight of its last, both included: `parisPeriodOf`, in `src/ui/paris-day-bounds.ts`, gives its two dates, and `periodBounds`, in `src/discord/period-bounds.ts` next to `snowflake-date.ts`, turns them into the `min_id` and the `max_id` that the search takes, as it does for the archives' page, which #394 moved it out of `src/ticket-archives/` for. The Discord adapter so imports nothing from `src/ui/`, which holds the Paris days (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #394, for Gilles to review, after the review of #394).

## Permissions

- Reading the game log asks View Channel and Read Message History on the channel, and the Message Content intent (ADR 0010). Gilles gave « Shor Panel » the first two on the category « 🛠️・LOG » on 2026-11-05, and « 🔧・log-jeux » and « 🔧・log-admin » are synced with it (#390, Gilles, 2026-11-05). ShorBot's audit of Shor on 2026-11-04 read « 🔧・log-jeux » as not synced, with an overwrite of its own that denies View Channel to @everyone (`discord-audit-shor.txt` of ShorBot, 2026-11-04), so the grant reaches the channel only while it stays synced to its category (**Channel visibility** of `CLAUDE.md`). Whether the page reads on Shor is for the first try on Shor to tell (#394; not tried).
- Without View Channel the channel is out of `listChannels` from 2026-11-16 and nobody views it for the panel, so everyone gets the not-found page; without Read Message History the search is inferred to answer `403` with code `50001`, as it does on the archives channel, and the page fails with the error page and an alert.

## Tests

- `src/game-log/` is tested on the fake Discord, then through `react-dom/server`: `gameLogMessage` poses a line in the format above and `gameLogChannel` a channel that given roles view, both test helpers that a `no-restricted-imports` rule of `eslint.config.mjs` keeps in test files. `src/game-log/read-game-log.test.ts` proves each filter, the term Discord is asked for and the ones it is not, the lines out of format, the forged line under a filter and the cut page, and `src/discord/scan-messages.test.ts` the loop under it. `e2e/game-log.test.ts` proves the link and the page for a staff member who views the channel and the not-found page for one who does not, a forged line left out, 25 lines then « Plus anciens » and back, the pills, each filter and the period in the address, the 360 px Sheet with the pills, its nine chips and their 44 px, the form inline at 768 and 1,024 px, the logout with the pills, « Réinitialiser », the pagination and the form in view and nothing prefetched, and Discord unavailable then « Réessayer ». That logout flow sets a viewport 6,000 px high so that the pagination and the form are both in view, since a page of 25 lines is taller than 3,600 px. `/game-log` is also crossed by `e2e/responsive.test.ts`, bare and with a category, an action, a character, a text and a period set so that its chips show, and by `e2e/skeletons.test.ts`.
- The links of a log never prefetch: the navigation's, the pagination's, the form's and the chips' take `prefetch={false}` (`next-link-prefetch.md`), and « Voir dans Discord » is a plain `<a>` to another origin.
