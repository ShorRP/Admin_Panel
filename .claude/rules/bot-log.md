# The Bot log's page (#396)

`/bot-log`, under « Journaux » in the navigation, lists what the Bot posts in « 🔧・log-admin » for each thing it does by itself (`GLOSSARY.md`, **Bot log**; ADR 0009, **The Bot log**; ADR 0010; #381). It is built on the log components of `src/ui/` (`CLAUDE.md`, **`src/ui/`**), as the audit log and the game log are, and on the game log's page for its access, its cursor and its link in the navigation (`game-log.md`). The embed's format is a contract with ShorBot that ADR 0009 holds, with the shared example `src/bot-log/bot-log-examples.json`. This file holds what a change to `src/bot-log/` must not break.

## Configuration and access

- `BOT_LOG_CHANNEL_ID` is the snowflake of the channel, and the only variable: the author the page keeps is the application, whose ID `getApplicationUser` gives, so the webhook's ID that the game log needs has no counterpart (`parseBotLogEnv`, `src/bot-log/env.ts`; both unset and empty count as unset, a value that is no snowflake is refused by the name of its variable, never its value). `next.config.ts` parses it at build when `VERCEL` is set.
- The values: « 🔧・log-admin » `1550837163464327289` on Shor, and « log-admin » `1568022878765719651` on Gilles' test Discord server (ADR 0009, #390). Setting the variable in Production and Preview on Vercel is Gilles' step, then a redeploy, since a variable reaches a deployment at its build (`env.md`).
- Access is the game log's: `readBotLogForStaff` answers `unconfigured` for a channel left out of the configuration, `hidden` unless the staff member views the channel (`viewsChannel`, **Channel visibility** of `CLAUDE.md`), and otherwise the page read; the page calls `notFound()` for `hidden`, inside the part under `<Suspense>`, so that the static `metadata` stays and the not-found page renders beside the navigation under the tab « Journal du Bot – Panel de Shor » (`e2e/bot-log.test.ts`). It reads the Discord server and its channels once and keeps the channels the staff member views, which it hands to the read for the names of channels (below). A Bot log left out of the configuration shows no link, and its page says « Le Journal du Bot n’est pas configuré pour ce serveur Discord. » to the whole staff.
- The layout gives `StaffHeader` a promise, `viewsBotLog`, from `viewsChannelOnceRead` of `src/staff/` (the function the game log's link uses, moved there and generalized by #396): « Journal du Bot » is the last item of the `logs` list, after « Journal du jeu », with the same `shown` promise.

## Reading

- `readBotLog(discord, { channelId }, directory, searchParams)`, in `src/bot-log/read-bot-log.ts`, asks Discord's search for the channel, the application as `author_id`, newest first, 25 a message page, and re-checks `author.id` and `channel_id` in memory, since Sky, a third-party bot, shares the channel until ShorBot#42 withdraws it and a forged line must not pass if the search lets it through (`src/bot-log/read-bot-log.test.ts`, through a fake that ignores `author_id`). It makes one `getApplicationUser` per view beside the searches.
- Pages go by cursor, as the game log's do: `before` holds the ID of the last line of a page, sent back as `max_id`, with the period's bounds from `searchBounds` (`src/discord/period-bounds.ts`), and « Plus anciens » shows when a page is full or cut, never from `total_results`.
- Discord's search serves one text, and the filters are five: the family, the action, the account, the channel and the text. So the read asks Discord for one term, the first of the account's ID, the channel's ID, the longest term of the text, the longest term of the action and the longest term of the family's name, and checks every filter again in memory, exactly, on the messages the search gives: the family on the title's family, the action by every word typed among the words of the title's action, whole and whatever the case, the account and the channel by their ID as a word of one text of the message, and the text by every word typed among the words of one text. The term sent is a necessary condition of each filter (the title holds the family and the action, a mention holds the ID), so Discord never hides a line that passes, and only one term is sent because Discord finds the words of one text of a message and never those of two (ADR 0010). That the real search finds a family's name in a title, and an ID in a field's value, was not tried on the real Discord server for this page: the archives' kinds and accounts rely on the same words (`.claude/rules/support-tickets.md`, **The filters**, #386), and the fake follows them (inferred; decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #396, for Gilles to review, since #396 says only that the filters sit in the URL and in the chips).
- A view makes up to 4 searches of 25 messages (`MAX_SEARCHES` of `src/discord/message-search-limits.ts`, which the record's archives and the support tickets' window read too), in `scanMessages` of `src/discord/scan-messages.ts`, which the game log's read uses too (#395), and keeps the lines that pass until it holds 25: one search for an unfiltered page, for a family or for a text, up to 4 for a combination Discord cannot serve whole, such as an account with a channel. When 4 searches leave fewer than 25 lines, the page is cut: it shows what it found, says that the 100 following messages of the channel were read (« Les 100 messages suivants du salon ont été lus, ce qui ne remplit pas la page »), or that none of them matched, and « Plus anciens » continues from the last message read. The 4 is the support tickets' window (`.claude/rules/support-tickets.md`, **The filters**), against the search's rate limit of about one call every 4 to 5 s after a burst (ADR 0010; not measured on this page). A page costs, beside those, a `getGuild` and a `listChannels` (**Channel visibility**), one `getMember` for each distinct account its lines name, up to about 75 for 25 lines of three accounts, and a `getUser` for each that has left, which are other routes (inferred not to count against the search's limit).
- An account or a channel typed as a name is resolved before anything is read: the account as the support tickets' opener is, by `resolveAccounts` against the members and the banned accounts, and the channel by `resolveChannel` among the channels the staff member views, by an ID, a mention, or a part of a name, whatever the case and a leading `#`, the one that is the whole name winning over those that contain it. None, or several, give no line and say so, with « Réinitialiser » (`BotLogNotice`), and the read makes no search. A channel the staff member does not view is not found by its name, and a deleted one, which the Bot's lines name after 5 s or a minute, is found by its ID or its mention (ADR 0009).
- A text and an action with no word in them count as absent, as the archives' reason does (`botLogFiltersOf`; **Validation**), as the game log's now do (#395, which changed #394's text with no word, sent to Discord to find nothing): the page checks the text in memory, which a text that matches everything would turn into a filter that does nothing (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #396, for Gilles to review).
- The time of a line is its embed's `timestamp`, which ADR 0009 sets as the time of the event, and the date of its message when it has none or when it is no date (`timeOf`, `src/bot-log/parse-bot-log-message.ts`); the page groups its days by that time, and `LogRow` shows it. The period and the cursor go by message IDs, which the search serves, and ShorBot posts a line seconds after the event, so a line stamped just before the edge of a period and posted just after it is in the period and shows under the day before it, and one stamped just after and posted before it is the other way round: the limit is the seconds the post takes, and the ADR names no tolerance (`src/bot-log/read-bot-log.test.ts`).
- A failure of the search, of the application's read or of a name's lookup is thrown and `error.tsx` replaces the page, as the support tickets' page does (**Read failures** of `CLAUDE.md`); a name that no account has is no failure, the ID shows in its place.

## The lines

- `parseBotLogMessage`, in `src/bot-log/parse-bot-log-message.ts`, reads a line in the format of ADR 0009: a message of one embed and no content whose title is `<family> · <action>` and is a row of the ADR's table, which `BOT_LOG_FAMILIES` of `bot-log-families.ts` holds (the five families and, for each, its actions; Serveurs de jeu and Sanctions automatiques have none yet). The fields come in the order Discord gives them, each value split into text and mentions (`<@id>`, `<@!id>`, `<@&id>`, `<#id>`, the ID in plain text beside each swallowed). A message that no row describes is a raw line, as the ADR says, « Tickets · Autre chose » included, so the PR that makes ShorBot publish a new event adds its row to the table, the twin of its ADR PR (`src/bot-log/parse-bot-log-message.test.ts` holds the table to the shared example, each example a row and each row an example). A description or an author name, which the format has none of, shows as extra text under the fields; the footer and the colour, which it leaves out of the contract, are not shown. A raw line shows its title, or its content when it has no embed, then each text as it was written (`rawMessageOf`, `src/discord/message-texts.ts`), still with its link to Discord.
- A line shows, from `BotLog` in `src/bot-log/bot-log.tsx`: its time in Paris, the tile of its family's icon (`BookOpenCheck` for Règlement, `Mic` for Vocaux, `Ticket` for Tickets, `Server` for Serveurs de jeu, `ShieldAlert` for Sanctions automatiques, `CircleHelp` for a raw line; the icons are the agent's, #396 asking for none), `<family> · <action>`, its fields on one line as `Name : value · Name : value`, and « Voir dans Discord » (`DiscordMessageLink` of `src/ui/`). A mention shows with the name Discord gives it: an account by `accountNames`, a nickname, else its name, a role by the Discord server's, a channel as `#name` when the staff member views it; a name the panel cannot find leaves the ID the line carries. For a staff member who moderates, each account is a `TextLink` to its record (`recordHrefForModerator`, in a sentence, so exempt from the 44 px), for the others plain text, and a role or a channel never a link.
- The skeleton draws six pill bars, the six fields' bars and five rows, on the real classes, as the game log's does.

## The filters

- `family`, `action`, `account`, `channel`, `text`, `from`, `to` and `before` are read from the address by `botLogFiltersOf`: `family` is one of `rules`, `voice-channels`, `tickets`, `game-servers` and `automatic-sanctions`, English slugs since a URL counts as a file name; `action`, `account` and `channel` are up to 100 characters; `text` holds 1,024 at most; days and the cursor are the shared `dayParamOf` and `cursorParamOf`. Anything invalid, empty, blank or repeated counts as absent. The address writes them in that order, which `botLogHref` gives the pills, the form, the Sheet, the chips and the pagination alike.
- The pills list the five families of ADR 0009, « Tout » first, two of them empty until ShorBot publishes Serveurs de jeu and Sanctions automatiques: the page then says that no line matches, and the pill finds the family's lines the day their rows join the table (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #396, for Gilles to review, #381's story 25 naming all five where ADR 0009 says that ShorBot publishes three today). A pill keeps the other filters and drops the cursor, and « Réinitialiser » shows once any filter is set.
- The fields are « Action », « Compte », « Salon », « Texte », « Du » and « Au », in that order, with the family in a hidden field so that sending the form keeps it. « Action » is a text of whole words, not a choice, so that an action ShorBot adds is found with no change here; it takes the words of the action, « activé » finding « Focus Mode activé » and not « Focus Mode désactivé » (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on #396, for Gilles to review, since #396 says only that the action is filtered). From `md` the form sits inline under the pills; under `md` it folds behind « Filtres », a button as wide as the column that opens a Sheet from the bottom, and the filters set show as chips, each a link to the address without that filter and without a cursor, the family among the filters kept and never a chip, since its pill says it (`FilterFold`).

## Permissions and personal data

- Reading the Bot log asks View Channel and Read Message History on « 🔧・log-admin » and the Message Content intent, for « Shor Panel »: Gilles gave the first two on the category « 🛠️・LOG » on 2026-11-05 and the channel is synced with it (#390). Without View Channel the channel is out of `listChannels` from 2026-11-16 and everyone gets the not-found page; without Read Message History the search is inferred to answer `403` with code `50001`, and the page fails with the error page and an alert (`game-log.md`). Whether the page reads on Shor is for the first try on Shor to tell (not tried).
- The page shows accounts, with their names, and the reasons that members typed to close a support ticket, which the Bot copies from its closing form (ADR 0009): `docs/personal-data.md` and `/privacy` say what the panel reads of it and keeps none (`.claude/rules/privacy-policy.md`, **Personal data**).

## Tests

- `src/bot-log/` is tested on the fake Discord, then through `react-dom/server`: `botLogMessage` poses a line from one of the shared examples, with the IDs and fields a test changes, and `botLogChannel` a channel that given roles view, both test helpers that a `no-restricted-imports` rule of `eslint.config.mjs` keeps in test files. `e2e/bot-log.test.ts` proves the link and the page for a staff member who views the channel, the names and the links to the records for a moderator and the plain text for the others, Sky's line left out, 25 lines then « Plus anciens » and back, each filter in the address, the not-found page for a staff member who does not view the channel, the logout with nothing prefetched, the Sheet and its chips at 360 px, the form inline at 768 and 1,024 px beside the rail and the sidebar, and Discord unavailable then « Réessayer ». `/bot-log` is also crossed by `e2e/responsive.test.ts` and `e2e/skeletons.test.ts`.
- The links of the page never prefetch: the navigation's, the pills', the pagination's, the form's, the chips' and the records' take `prefetch={false}` (`next-link-prefetch.md`), and « Voir dans Discord » is a plain `<a>` to another origin.
