# Gotchas of `next/link` and `next/form` prefetches

- Under `next start` on `http://localhost`, and presumably under `next dev` (inferred, not tried), Chromium sends to `https:` the target of a redirect that a `fetch` follows, under the Content Security Policy's `upgrade-insecure-requests`, while it leaves on `http:` what a page requests from `localhost` itself (`.claude/rules/nextjs.md`, **Headers**): on 2026-10-27, a `next/link` to `/` failed its RSC `fetch` on `https://localhost:3100/blocked-words?_rsc=…` with `ERR_SSL_PROTOCOL_ERROR`, and Next.js logged « Failed to fetch RSC payload for http://localhost:3100/. Falling back to browser navigation. », which fails end to end (**The browser's console**), while a plain `<a href="/">` reached `/blocked-words` over `http:` (throwaway Playwright tests). So the panel's link to `/`, which always redirects, is a plain `<a>`. A section's `next/link` logged the same error once its prefetch reached `/login` right after a logout, in 3 runs of 24 of the header flow on `main` (#153, 2026-10-28), so the sections' links do not prefetch (**Navigation**). The audit log's « Revenir aux plus récents » and « Plus anciens » met it too (`docs/audit-log.md`, **Trials**, 2026-10-30, #168). So they do not prefetch either (`.claude/rules/audit-log.md`, **No prefetch and end to end**, #168), nor do the type filter's pills, « Réinitialiser » and the link of a sanction to its account's record, each of which turns the flow of #268 red when its `prefetch={false}` is taken out (8 runs of 8 each, `docs/audit-log.md`, **Trials**, 2026-10-30), nor does the audit log's filter form, a `next/form`, which turns it red too (8 runs of 8, `docs/audit-log.md`, **Trials**, 2026-11-02, #321), nor do the `AccountLink`s, which draw the audit log's author link and the identity's links in the sidebar and the bar, nor the identity's link in the menu, which turn the flows that cover them red when their `prefetch={false}` is taken out (8 runs of 8 each, **End to end** and `docs/audit-log.md`, **Trials**, 2026-11-02, #320), and no other `next/link` or `next/form` of `src/` does (read one by one, 2026-10-30, and again on 2026-11-02, #321 and #320): one added without `prefetch={false}` to a page that `proxy.ts` redirects without a session could meet it after a logout (inferred, not tried). Since #256, « Réinitialiser », « Tout afficher » and a sanction's link to its account's record are written with `TextLink`, which sets `prefetch={false}` itself, so a link written as one cannot meet it; the trials above ran on the `next/link`s they were before, and none ran with that prop taken out of `TextLink` (inferred, #256). While the page stays, a link to its own pathname sends no prefetch request: a throwaway Playwright probe that timed each prefetch `fetch` of the audit log's middle page logged none before the logout's response, and two 306 to 311 ms after it, in 8 runs of 8 with the page's script held, the router having emptied its cache by then (2026-10-30; **Navigation** for the cooldown). Over Vercel's `https:` nothing needs upgrading (inferred).
