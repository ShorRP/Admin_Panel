# Hydromel mockup captures

These captures are visual references for the redesign of #221, taken from the mockup Gilles made with Claude Design (#221). They are no specification: the decisions are on #221 and its tickets, and where a capture and a ticket differ, the ticket wins. The mockup contradicts `CLAUDE.md` in many points, which the tickets settle one by one, so the repository holds its captures rather than the mockup itself, whose generated runtime is full of comments that `pnpm check:comments` would refuse (inferred from the comment lines that `grep -c` counts in its scripts, #223). The captures show, for one, the counter « 0/60 » in the blocked word's field, the staff filter « Tout le staff » of the audit log and the copy button beside a record's ID, all three of which #221 refuses.

## Where they come from

- The mockup, `Panel Shor.dc.html`, sits in the handoff folder that Claude Design produced for Gilles, under `handoff/maquette/`, on his machine and outside the repository (#221). The tickets cite its lines as `M:n`, the line `n` of that file.
- Its `palette` property was on « Hydromel » and its `navLayout` on « Sidebar », their defaults (M:618).
- It ran in the Claude app's built-in browser, the one place #223 lets it run, in a window emulated at 1440 × 900 px, from a local static server that served only the files of `handoff/maquette/` and received the PNGs. Light and dark come from the mockup's own toggle. A PNG is the page's DOM drawn as an SVG `foreignObject` image on a canvas, at scale 1, then encoded as PNG: its width is the page's `document.documentElement.clientWidth`, and its height the document's `scrollHeight`, both read before the render (the orchestrating session's capture run, 2026-10-29).
- The pages that fit in 900 px are 1440 × 900 px, while `audit-log`, at 1425 × 1475, and `record-member` with its tabs and dialogs, at 1425 × 1043, are 15 px narrower (the PNGs' headers, 2026-10-29), the width of the vertical scrollbar that `clientWidth` leaves out (inferred from `clientWidth`'s definition).
- The accounts, the staff members, the blocked words and the dates are the mockup's sample data (M:621, M:627-661, M:673).
- In the left column of the login pages, the mockup leaves the slot of its illustration empty (`auth-art`, M:32), which #236 does not take.
