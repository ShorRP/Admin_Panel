# The panel reads the ticket archives from Discord

The Admin Panel shows an account's support tickets on its record (#197) by reading the ticket archives that ShorBot posts in the archives channel, « 📃・transcriptions » on Shor, through Discord's REST API with the token of the application they share (ADR 0008). ShorBot sends the panel nothing, and the panel keeps no copy: no Route Handler, no shared secret, no row in the store. Decided by Gilles on 2026-11-04, while grilling ShorRP/ShorBot#26, since nothing says the panel will ever have a store for the archives (Gilles, 2026-11-03, on #197), and Discord already keeps them; ShorBot's ADR 0008 records it on its side.

The embed's title, « 🗄️ ARCHIVE D'UN TICKET », its six field names, « Ouvert par », « Fermé par », « Type de demande », « Ouvert le », « Fermé le » and « Motif », the ID in plain text beside each mention, the request kind's emoji and label or « Inconnu », and the `<t:…:F>` timestamps are a contract between the two repositories: a change to one of them lands in both, in two PRs that name each other. The four request kinds of ShorBot's `REQUEST_KINDS`, each with its emoji and its label, join the contract on 2026-11-05: the panel's page of archives filters by kind and counts them, and it finds a kind by searching its emoji (#386). ShorRP/ShorBot#91 lists the kinds in ShorBot's ADR 0008. A kind that ShorBot adds shows in the list and in "Tout", with no pill of its own, until the panel adds it.

## Considered Options

- **ShorBot posting each archive to a Route Handler of the panel, authenticated by a shared secret, into the store** (ADR 0003, 2026-10-28): it waited on a store nobody has planned (#174), and added a secret on two hosts and a second copy of the archive.
- **No archive on the record**: « 📃・transcriptions » alone.

## Consequences

- #197 no longer waits on the store (#174, #175). Its retention is the message's: an archive stays until a staff member deletes it, which is also how one is erased.
- The panel's configuration holds the archives channel's ID for each Discord server, beside ShorBot's.
- The panel finds an account's archives through Discord's guild message search, whose text is the account's ID (#387, 2026-11-05). The embed holds that ID in the field of its opener and in that of its closer, and the search cannot tell the fields apart (ADR 0010), so the panel keeps the archives the account opened. It reads the 100 newest messages that cite the account at most, 4 searches of 25, since the search allows about one call every 4 to 5 s, and the record says so when more cite it, which a staff member who closed many tickets can meet. The search needs the Message Content intent (https://docs.discord.com/developers/change-log, 2026-03-19; ADR 0010). The panel first paged the channel's history, 100 messages a call and 10 calls at most, which needs no privileged intent, and did not read past the 1,000 newest messages (#197).
- The archive holds no conversation: the support ticket's channel and its messages are gone 5 seconds after its closing (ShorRP/ShorBot#23).

## The support tickets in progress

Added by #388 on 2026-11-05, from the grilling of #380 (Gilles). The page `/support-tickets` also lists the support tickets still open, read at each view from the tickets' category with the same REST API and the same application (ADR 0008), and kept nowhere (ADR 0010). What the panel reads of them is a contract with ShorBot, as the archive's embed is, and ShorBot's side of it is the section « The support tickets in progress » of its ADR 0008 (the twin PR ShorRP/ShorBot#91): a change to one of the points below lands in both repositories, in two PRs that name each other.

**Facts**, from `shorbot/support_tickets.py`, `shorbot/config.py` and `shorbot/discord_adapter.py` on `dev-gilles`, commit 9915eb7, read on 2026-11-05:

- The tickets are channels of one category, `TICKET_CATEGORY_ID` (`config.py:20`, `1559574907426439229` on Shor), which holds 50 channels at most: `CATEGORY_LIMIT` (`support_tickets.py:53`), checked before a ticket opens (`:328`).
- A ticket's channel is named `{emoji}・{name}`, the separator being U+30FB (`NAME_SEPARATOR`, `:54`), as `_open` writes it (`:330-335`) and `kind_of` reads it, by `startswith` over the kinds in order (`:150-158`).
- The four request kinds are 🔧 Problème technique, 🚨 Signaler un joueur, 🎭 Demande RP and ❓ Autre demande (`REQUEST_KINDS`, `config.py:33-70`), written `{emoji} {label}` in an archive (`kind_label`, `support_tickets.py:112-113`).
- The opener is the one non-role target with an overwrite that allows View Channel: `_opener_id` (`discord_adapter.py:139-145`) gives none for zero or several, and `ticket_category` leaves a channel with no opener out of its tickets (`:447-460`).
- `create_support_ticket_channel` writes the overwrites (`:462-483`): `@everyone` cannot view the channel (`TICKET_HIDDEN`), the application's own role can (`TICKET_OWN_ROLE`), and the opener and the roles of the request kind (`role_ids_by_kind`, `support_tickets.py:313`) get `TICKET_PARTICIPANT` (`discord_adapter.py:35-42`). The channel is not synced with its category.

**What the panel does with them**

- A channel is a ticket when it is a text channel whose `parent_id` is the configured category, whose name starts with the emoji of one of the four kinds and the separator, and that has exactly one overwrite of a member allowing View Channel, the opener's. Any other channel of the category is left out. ShorBot's `ticket_category` would also keep a channel whose name names no kind, which the panel does not, since the page shows the request kind (#388, from the ticket's « ne suit pas le nom de ShorBot »).
- It shows a ticket to a staff member only if they view its channel on Discord, which it works out from the channel's own overwrites and never from its category's, since the channel is not synced with it (`viewsChannel`, #384).
- It reads the last 100 messages of each ticket, with the Message Content intent (ADR 0010), which empties the content and the embeds of another author's message without it.

**Inferences**

- From 2026-11-16, `GET /guilds/{guild.id}/channels` omits the channels the application cannot view (https://docs.discord.com/developers/change-log, 12 August 2026, read 2026-11-05), so the tickets stay listed to the application only while `TICKET_OWN_ROLE` stays in each channel's overwrites: the application's role holds View Channel on a ticket's channel by that overwrite and not by its category's, which a ticket is not synced with (#384, #382).
- The ticket's opener is told apart from the roles by the overwrite's type, 1 for a member and 0 for a role (`permission_overwrites`), the type that `_is_role` tests on discord.py's side.

## The Bot log

Added by ShorRP/ShorBot#87 on 2026-11-06, and extended by ShorRP/ShorBot#89 with the Tickets events (the twin PR ShorRP/ShorBot#93), from the grilling of the logs' pages (#381, Gilles, 2026-11-05). The page `/bot-log` (#396) reads « 🔧・log-admin », where the Bot posts an embed for each thing it does by itself, with no staff member behind it, through the same REST API and the same application (ADR 0008) and Discord's message search (ADR 0010), and keeps no copy. The embed's format is a contract between the two repositories, as the archive's embed is: ShorBot's side of it is the section « The Bot log » of its ADR 0008 (the twin PR ShorRP/ShorBot#92), and a change to one of the points below lands in both repositories, in two PRs that name each other. The rows of the Vocaux family were added by ShorRP/ShorBot#88 on 2026-11-06 (the twin PR ShorRP/ShorBot#94).

- **The channel.** « 🔧・log-admin », `1550837163464327289` on Shor and `1568022878765719651` on the test Discord server, which the panel's configuration does not hold yet: #396 puts them in it, beside ShorBot's. Sky, a third-party bot, posts there today and is to be withdrawn (ShorRP/ShorBot#42), so the panel keeps only the messages whose author is the application (ADR 0010).
- **One event, one message.** A message holds one embed and no content.
- **The title** is `<family> · <action>`, the two joined by a space, U+00B7 and a space. The families are Règlement, Vocaux, Tickets, Serveurs de jeu and Sanctions automatiques. The actions of a family are the rows of the table below: the PR that makes ShorBot publish a new event adds its row to both ADRs.
- **Named fields.** Each event has the fields of its row, in that order, all inline, each with a name and a value.
- **The ID in plain text beside each mention**, as in the archive: a field's value holds `<@id> (`id`)` for a member, `<@&id> (`id`)` for a role and `<#id> (`id`)` for a channel, so that a filter finds the account or the channel when the mention no longer shows a name.
- **The time of the event** is the embed's `timestamp`, in ISO 8601 with an offset, read from the Bot's clock when the Bot publishes the event, right after the action it records returned, which can differ from the time of the message by the time the post takes.
- **Not in the contract:** the embed's colour and its footer, « Shor — journal du Bot ».
- **A line that does not follow the format** is a message of the application that no row describes. The page shows it as it is (#396).

| Title                           | Fields                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Règlement · Rôle Membre donné` | « Membre », the member who got the role; « Rôle », the Membre role; « Déclencheur », the text « Réaction ✅ » when the member reacted, or « Rattrapage au démarrage » when the Bot gave the role at start-up to a member who had reacted while it was stopped.                                                                                    |
| `Vocaux · Vocal créé`           | « Propriétaire », the member whose temporary voice channel it is, the one who joined the creation channel; « Salon », the temporary voice channel.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `Vocaux · Vocal supprimé`       | « Propriétaire » and « Salon », as above. The Bot deletes the channel by itself, after a minute with nobody in it, so the owner is the one the channel had and not someone who acted; the mention of a deleted channel shows no name, and the ID beside it is what is left.                                                                       |
| `Vocaux · Focus Mode activé`    | « Propriétaire », the owner who turned Focus Mode on; « Salon », the temporary voice channel.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `Vocaux · Focus Mode désactivé` | « Propriétaire », the owner who turned Focus Mode off; « Salon », the temporary voice channel.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `Vocaux · Membre expulsé`       | « Propriétaire », the owner who expelled the member; « Salon », the temporary voice channel; « Membre expulsé », the member expelled from it.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `Tickets · Ticket ouvert`       | « Type de demande », the request kind as the archive writes it, the emoji and the label (« 🔧 Problème technique »); « Ouvert par », the member who opened the support ticket; « Salon », the support ticket's channel.                                                                                                                           |
| `Tickets · Ticket fermé`        | The three fields of the opening, in that order, then « Fermé par », the member who closed the support ticket, the opener or a staff member of its request kind; « Motif », the reason typed in the closing form, trimmed, as free text with no ID. « Type de demande » is « Inconnu » when a staff member renamed the channel, as in the archive. |

- **The shared example.** `src/bot-log/bot-log-examples.json` here and `tests/bot_log_examples.json` in ShorBot are one file, byte for byte: one embed for each event of the table, as Discord returns it without `type` and `flags`, under a name of its own. `src/bot-log/bot-log-examples.test.ts` checks on every example the title, that every field is inline, the ID beside each mention and a timestamp with an offset, and #396 builds its test helper, `bot-log-message.ts`, on the file, as `archive-message.ts` reproduces ShorBot's `archive_embed`. A change to a row changes the example in both repositories.

**Consequences**

- Règlement has one event: ShorBot never takes the Membre role away, since removing ✅ keeps it and only the staff removes a role (ShorRP/ShorBot#7), so there is no « retiré » row, where #381's story 1 and ShorBot's #87 say « donné ou retiré » (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on ShorRP/ShorBot#87, for Gilles to review). A row for it comes with a ShorBot function that removes the role.
- Vocaux has five events for the four actions of ShorRP/ShorBot#88, delivered by ShorRP/ShorBot#94 (creation, deletion, Focus Mode, expulsion): turning Focus Mode on and turning it off are two rows, so that the page filters by action (ShorRP/ShorBot#86, story 7; decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on ShorRP/ShorBot#88, for Gilles to review). Both creation channels, the open one and the staff-only one, publish the same rows, and ShorBot publishes nothing for an action it did not do (a click of a member who is not the owner, a refused choice, an action Discord refused).
- Tickets has two events, one for each of the ends of a support ticket that ShorBot carries out: the opening, once the channel and its first message exist, and the closing, once the archive is posted and the closing message written (ShorRP/ShorBot#89). The closing event names the channel by a mention that no longer resolves once the channel is deleted, 5 seconds later, which is why the ID is in plain text beside it. The archive stays the record of the closing, in the archives channel: the event is the Bot log's own line, and the page of the support tickets reads the archives alone. The fields, their order and the archive's words for them are the agent's choice, since the criteria of ShorRP/ShorBot#89 name what the events carry, not how (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on ShorRP/ShorBot#89, for Gilles to review). So is the reading of the ticket's « chacun avec son ID »: the ID in plain text beside each mention, the opener, the closer and the channel, as the format above says, where the reason is free text and carries none (decided by the agent on 2026-11-06, on ShorRP/ShorBot#89, for Gilles to review).
- A publication that fails never fails what ShorBot did (Gilles, 2026-11-05, ShorRP/ShorBot#86, story 10): the event is lost, and ShorBot alerts. The page shows no line for an event ShorBot failed to publish.
- The panel reads the channel as it reads the game log's, with the Message Content intent through REST (ADR 0010), and the Bot reads nothing of it (ShorBot's ADR 0004). `/privacy` and the panel's record list the panel's reading with #396; what ShorBot publishes there is in ShorBot's record, and ShorBot's part of `/privacy` is Gilles' to decide (ShorRP/ShorBot#74).
