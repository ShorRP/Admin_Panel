# The panel reads the Discord server's channels with the Message Content intent, and keeps no copy

The Admin Panel shows the game log, the support tickets in progress and every ticket archive, all read from Discord's channels through its REST API, and filters them through Discord's guild message search rather than a store of its own, as ADR 0004 and ADR 0009 already keep the audit log and the archives in Discord. The game log is posted by another author, a webhook of the game server (inferred from its author, « ShorAPP », which holds no role on Shor, `discord-audit-shor.txt` of ShorBot, 2026-11-04), and a support ticket's messages by the members and the staff, so the panel needs the Message Content intent of the Discord application it shares with the Bot (ADR 0008), without which Discord empties the content and the embeds of a message another author posted; the search requires it too (https://docs.discord.com/developers/change-log, 2026-03-19, as ADR 0009 reads it). Decided by Gilles on 2026-11-05, while grilling the logs' pages and the support tickets' page.

The intent is turned on in the Developer Portal for « Shor Panel » and « Shor Panel Dev ». The panel uses it through REST alone: the Bot does not ask for it when it connects to the gateway, so ShorBot's ADR 0004 holds and the Bot still reads no message's text.

The game log's format, the title `<category> · <action>`, its fields and its footer `Shor · <hash>`, is a contract between the game server and the panel, as the archive's embed is between the Bot and the panel (ADR 0009). Anyone who holds the webhook's URL can post a line in its format, so the panel keeps only the messages whose author is that webhook's ID, which its configuration holds beside the channel's, as it keeps only the Bot log's messages whose author is the application.

## Considered Options

- **A store fed by the panel or by the Bot** (Neon, #174, #175): a second copy of what Discord keeps, with its retention and its personal data, for a filter Discord's search gives.
- **Reading the channels' history page by page and filtering in memory**, as a record read its archives until #387 (ADR 0009): bounded by the pages read, 1,000 messages for an archive, where a log channel grows by the hour.

## Consequences

- `docs/personal-data.md` and `/privacy` say what the panel now reads without keeping: the game log's characters, game accounts and places, and the support tickets' conversations in progress.
- The search reads the text of an embed, which holds the whole of the game log and of an archive, and serves the filters listed under **What the search does**; a filter it cannot serve is done in memory over the pages read.
- The application needs View Channel and Read Message History on the channels it reads, which Gilles grants on Shor.

## What the search does

Established by #383 on 2026-11-05, for `GET /guilds/{guild.id}/messages/search` (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/message, **Search Guild Messages**, read 2026-11-05), on Gilles' test Discord server, with « Shor Panel Dev », whose Message Content intent is on (`flags` 557056, #382). The sources are throwaway scripts of that day, `probe-383-1.mjs` to `probe-383-10.mjs` and `probe-383-webhook.mjs`, kept outside the repository, and `pnpm test:contract`. Unless a fact says otherwise, a probe posted the test messages it names in « transcriptions » (`1567881753266229298`), searched them and deleted them, and the contract suite posted its six in the first text channel the application reads and posts in, « général » (`1563189323476504598`), and deleted them (42 tests of 42 on the second and the third full runs, the first having failed one test, below).

**Facts**

- The search reads the content of a message and the text of its embeds: the title, the description, the author's name, the name and the value of a field, the footer. `probe-383-6.mjs` posted an embed holding a distinct word in each of those, and each word found that message alone; the contract suite does the same.
- A word is found whole, whatever its case, with the accents it was written with, and never cut short. `probe-383-6.mjs` found a capitalized word by its lower case and its upper case, an accented word by itself and by nothing without its accent, and no word by its first five letters or its last six. The contract suite proves the case, the accent and the first five letters.
- A hyphen splits words, a number or an ID is a word, an emoji alone is a word. `probe-383-6.mjs`: `4242` found both a message holding `ticket-4242` and a message holding `nombre 4242`. `probe-383-8.mjs` found archives, posted in the shape of `archiveEmbed`, by the ID of an account in the field « Ouvert par » or « Fermé par », whose value is `<@id> (` then the ID in backticks. `probe-383-7.mjs` found an archive by the emoji `🔧` of its request kind.
- Several words are found when they sit close together in one text, in any order. `probe-383-6.mjs`: two words swapped, and two words with one between them, found their message, and the second did not with `slop` set to 0. The docs give `slop` as the « Max number of words to skip between matching tokens (max 100, default 2) »; nothing measured a gap of two or three words. The contract suite proves two words swapped.
- Two texts are never one: `probe-383-6.mjs` found nothing for a word of a message's content and a word of its embed, nor for two words of two messages, and `probe-383-9.mjs` found nothing for two words that follow each other across the end of a field's value and the next field's name, a field's name and its value, a description and the footer, the end of an embed and the start of the next, a content and an embed, where two words of one field's value or of one content were found. The contract suite proves the content and the embed of one message.
- `channel_id`, `author_id`, `min_id`, `max_id`, `limit`, `offset` and `sort_order` are served, as the contract suite proves with six messages: the application's messages are found by its ID and a member's by none, `limit` and `offset` cut the pages and `asc` reverses them. `author_type`, `has` and `sort_by` are served too, which the panel does not use and the contract suite does not try: `probe-383-6.mjs` found the six messages with `author_type` set to `bot` and none with `-bot` or `user` or `webhook`, the two with an embed with `has` set to `embed` and the four others with `-embed`, and another order with `sort_by` set to `relevance`.
- `min_id` and `max_id` are strict bounds: a message is left out by the `min_id` and by the `max_id` that name it (`probe-383-6.mjs`; the contract suite).
- `author_id` serves a webhook's ID. `probe-383-webhook.mjs` ran on 2026-11-05 against a message that Gilles posted through a webhook he made in « transcriptions », « Transcription Hook Test » (`1567897986048983050`), with the content `Probe383 kafuvobeki` and the embed description `Embed rutelosami`. It found that message, and it alone, by the content word, by the embed word, by `author_id` set to the webhook's ID, with the channel and with none, and by `author_type` set to `webhook`, and found none with `author_type` set to `bot` or `-webhook`. The author of the message found has the webhook's ID, and so does its `webhook_id`.
- A channel the application cannot view and a channel that does not exist are both refused with a 403 of code 50001 when alone in `channel_id` (`probe-383-6.mjs`, with `100000000000000000` and with « salon-privé », `1564046605156487348`, which the application cannot view; the contract suite proves the first). With another channel beside it, the channel the application cannot view is left out: `probe-383-6.mjs` gave a 200 with the messages of « transcriptions » alone.
- A `limit` of 26 or 0, an `offset` of 9,976 or -1, a snowflake that is `abc`, and a text of 1,025 characters are refused with a 400 of code 50035, and an `offset` of 9,975 gives a 200 with no message (`probe-383-6.mjs`). So are a `limit` or an `offset` that is `abc`, `NaN` or a decimal (`probe-383-10.mjs`). The contract suite proves a `limit` of 26 and 2.5, an `offset` of 9,976 and 1.5, `NaN` for both, and a text of 1,025 characters. A blank text filters nothing, and a text with no word, `«»`, finds nothing (`probe-383-6.mjs`).
- `total_results` counts every page, not the page, and each message found comes in an array of its own, flagged `hit` (`probe-383-6.mjs` with `limit` set to 2; `probe-383-2.mjs`, whose seven results came in seven arrays of one message; `probe-383-3.mjs`, the keys of a message found; the contract suite for the total).
- An opener cannot be told from a closer, and a text cannot be limited to a field. `probe-383-7.mjs`: the ID of the member who opened one archive and closed another found both, and the filter `mentions` set to that member found none, though the field « Ouvert par » mentions them.
- The index lags. `probe-383-3.mjs`: a content word was found 35 s after its post. `probe-383-4.mjs`: three messages were listed between 8 and 23 s after their post, one of their words being found at 8 s. `probe-383-6.mjs`: six messages were all listed 57 s after their posts, and the words searched 20 s later were all found. `probe-383-7.mjs`: three messages were listed at 24 s, and at 39 s a search found an ID in a field and not the ID in another field, nor the word « archive » of the title. `probe-383-8.mjs`, the same fixture posted again: 15 queries gave the same answers at 126 s, 214 s and 304 s. `probe-383-9.mjs`: listed at 39 s.
- A search can give fewer messages than it should. In the first full run of the contract suite, on 2026-11-05, minutes after the post, the search with `min_id` and `max_id` returned one message where the six were all found a moment before and two were expected, and the second full run found both when every expectation was retried. The docs warn of it: « search may return slightly fewer results than the limit specified when messages have not been accessed for a long time », and « Clients should not rely on the length of the `messages` array to paginate results » (**Search Guild Messages**, read 2026-11-05). The contract suite retries what it expects a search to find every 5 s for 4 minutes.
- The rate limit is of the `shared` scope for the most part. `probe-383-5.mjs`: after 12 s of idle, 10 searches back to back gave 7 answers and 3 429s, of `retry_after` 1.2, 0.3 and 0.8 s, the first and the third `shared` and the second `user`; then one search a second gave 4 answers and a 429 of 4.4 s, then 3.2, 2.0 and 0.8 s, one answer, and 429s of 4.4, 3.2 and 2.0 s again. `X-RateLimit-Limit` was 6. Every probe that searched at a steady pace read the same, a 429 of about 4.2 s for each search past a burst of a few. So the search allows about one call every 4 to 5 s once the burst has passed.
- Its index was ready, and none of the searches of the ten probes, of the webhook probe or of the three full runs of the contract suite answered 202: the first search of « transcriptions » (`probe-383-1.mjs`) and the first of the whole Discord server (`probe-383-2.mjs`) answered 200 at once, with `doing_deep_historical_index` false.

**Inferences**

- A message just deleted may still count in `total_results` for a few seconds: the first search of `probe-383-4.mjs` read a total of 3 with none of its own three messages listed, which are inferred to be the three that `probe-383-3.mjs` had deleted shortly before, and read 0 eight seconds later.
- The Discord server of Shor may answer 202 with a `retry_after` at its first search, if its index is not ready: the docs say so for an entity that is not indexed (« Index not yet available. Try again later », code 110000), and the test Discord server could not show it. `searchMessages` retries a 202 once, then fails as Discord unavailable (`CLAUDE.md`, **Retries**).
- With the Message Content intent off, Discord empties the content and the embeds of a message another author posted, unless the application wrote it or it mentions the application (https://docs.discord.com/developers/events/gateway, **Message Content Intent**, read 2026-11-05). Whether the search then still finds such a message by its text was not tried, since the intent is on for both applications; the fake finds it by no text (`CLAUDE.md`, **The fake**).
- Words split at what is not a letter, a digit or an emoji, and two words found a gap of two words apart, are generalizations of the facts above, which `probe-383-6.mjs` and `probe-383-7.mjs` showed for a hyphen, a number, an ID, an emoji, a swap and a gap of one word.
- A period is served only by the time of the message, which for an archive is the time the Bot posted it, when the ticket closed (ADR 0009), not the time the ticket opened. So a filter on the opening, on the opener alone, on the closer alone, on the kind of request or on the reason alone is made in memory over the pages read, by #386 for the archives' filters, by #385 for their page, by #387 for a record's, and by #381 for the logs.

**Constraint for #381, #385, #386 and #387**

The search allows about one call every 4 to 5 s, once a burst of a few has passed, and answers a call over it with a 429 whose `retry_after` reached 4.4 s, where the client waits for a `retry_after` of 2 s or less (`CLAUDE.md`, **Retries**). A page that searches several times in one view, or two views within a few seconds, can fail on the second 429 as Discord unavailable. The tickets that read the search count their searches per view against that rate, and know whether another page exists from `total_results` and the offset, not from the length of a page.
