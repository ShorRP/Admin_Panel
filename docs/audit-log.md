# Audit log

The reference behind `.claude/rules/audit-log.md`: what a view costs in calls to Discord, where the page departs from the mockup, and the measures and trials behind some choices. The rule says what to do; this file says how it was known.

The `node_modules` citations are those of Base UI 1.9.0: an update of a package re-reads the citations that name it (**Docs that name a version** of `CLAUDE.md`).

## Calls per view

- The page makes, at each view, one call to the audit log for each kind of entry it reads (`EVENTS_OF_TYPE`, `src/audit-log/read-audit-log.ts:108-116`), seven in all: the two kinds of the panel's rules only once one of those rules exists, since the changes of every rule of the panel are kept in memory by the rule IDs the AutoMod rules give (`read-audit-log.ts:240-246`, `:265-267`; #325), and AutoMod's timeouts, `AUTO_MODERATION_USER_COMMUNICATION_DISABLED`, always, since they name no rule of the panel and are read whichever rule gave them (#327). One more call for each further 100 entries of a kind, one after another (`audit-log-pages.ts:13-35`), until the read holds more than the page's 50 changes of that kind, or more than 100 entries of one of the panel's rules, whose change of action writes two that the page reads as one, or reaches Discord's 45 days (`read-audit-log.ts:123-125`, `:324-326`; #182, #326).
- Filtered by an author, an account, a text or a period, it makes the same calls for the kinds it reads, but counts toward the page's 50 only the changes that meet the filters (`retainerOf`, `read-audit-log.ts:177-193`), so that a rare author, account or text, or a period far back, can have it read every page of Discord's 45 days for each kind, where an unfiltered page stops at 50 changes of a kind (#321, #393; inferred, not tried against Discord's own pages).
- Filtered by a type, it makes the calls of that type's kinds alone, and reads the AutoMod rules, which give it the IDs of the panel's rules, only where a rule kind is read, as a read that Discord answers for one type alone shows (`src/audit-log/read-audit-log.test.ts`, #231, #327).
- `readAuditLog` makes one call to `GET /users/@me`, `getApplicationUser`, beside the AutoMod rules (`read-audit-log.ts:229-234`), since `attributionOf` counts an entry as made through the panel only if its `user_id` is the application's and its reason follows the panel's format (`src/audit-log/entry-attribution.ts:27-53`; #210). It makes none when the read reads nothing (`read-audit-log.ts:226-228`, `:143-145`). A record's history makes it too, before its other reads (`src/sanctions/read-sanction-history.ts:47`, `docs/sanctions.md`, **The history**), and a record loaded in full reads it a second time for `readSanctionRefusals` (`src/sanctions/sanction-refusal.ts:44`), with no request-scoped `cache()` to share it, as for `getMember` (**Navigation** of `CLAUDE.md`) (decided by the agent on 2026-10-30, on #210, for Gilles to review).
- The page's avatars add one `getMember` per distinct member of the page, up to 50 at once (`read-audit-log.ts:154-157`, `:282-295`), and count toward the same 429 as the staff check's, with no ticket of their own: they turn their own 429 into initials, and whether they could leave the next staff check a 429 longer than the 2 s it waits (`.claude/rules/discord.md`, **Errors and retries**) is inferred, not tried (Gilles, 2026-10-28, on #141).

## Where the page departs from the mockup

The mockup's lines are quoted as `M:<line>`, from the captures of `docs/design/hydromel/`.

- A sanction's account is a link to its record only for a staff member who moderates, where the mockup shows it to all (#182, #221).
- « Revenir aux plus récents » and « Plus anciens » show only where they lead somewhere, as before #230, which reads « Il y a toujours » as « still there », against the mockup that drops the first (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #230, for Gilles to review; `src/ui/log-pagination.tsx:28-55`).
- The pills of the types take the mockup's look (M:278-282, M:922-925), but a hover on `accent` where the mockup's take none (M:280) (the hover decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #231, for Gilles to review).
- « Réinitialiser » is a link drawn as « Tout afficher », through `TextLink`, in `foreground` where the mockup draws it in `--fg2` (M:285), beside the pills when a filter is set and under an empty page again, as a link where the mockup draws a button « Réinitialiser les filtres » (M:354-358) (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #231, for Gilles to review; `audit-log-type-filter.test.tsx:52`, `:74`).
- The mockup's « Tout le staff », a filter by staff member, stayed out of #231, since `DiscordApi` lists no staff and a change made through the panel names its staff member only in its reason (#221, #231). #321 lifts that: the filter reads the author in that reason, or in Discord's audit log, and lists nobody (#317, #321).
- Each page groups its own 50 changes by day of Paris, so a day cut between two pages heads both, and a day has no « Aujourd’hui », « Hier » or « Lundi » and no count of its changes, which would be wrong for it (`read-audit-log.test.ts`, `src/ui/log-day.test.tsx:6-19`; #221, #230).
- The icons of a row, their tile and the grid of its cells are the mockup's (M:324-331, M:846, M:268-361).
- The box of 1,080 px is centered, as that of `/blocked-words` and the mockup's (M:174), and the header holds `max-w-160` (decided by the agent on 2026-10-29, on #230, for Gilles to review, since `/records` did not center before #234).

## Measures

- The skeleton's rows, measured by a throwaway Playwright probe over `holdDiscord` at 1,280, 640, 639 and 360 px (2026-11-02, #319): from 640 px the centers of the time's cell, the tile, the title's block, the staff member's cell and the circle all sat on the row's middle, while at 639 and 360 px those of the time, the tile, the staff member and the circle shared one axis, with the title's block starting 10 px under the tile's bottom. No test of the repository measures them; the skeleton shares the real row's classes.
- The real row, in `e2e/audit-log.test.ts` (`share one axis`): the flow fixes both widths, reads every box of a row in one `evaluate`, so that a late font cannot move the row between two reads, compares each center with the row's middle at 1,280 px and with the tile's at 360 px within 1 px, and the time's, the tile's and the avatar's centers among themselves within 1 px at both, taking the row's middle from its box less its `divide-y` border. It failed on a build of ef70f50's sources, on the one-line row's time, 2 px off the middle at 1,280 px, and passed 8 runs of 8 with `--repeat-each 8` on #319's layout (2026-11-02).
- The icon of a row is decorative, since the title says the kind, so 1.4.11 asks no contrast of its color (inferred from https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html).
- The pagination's links take the whole width under `sm`, side by side in two equal columns when both show, each wrapping its text within its 44 px, where « Revenir aux plus récents » takes two lines in 156 px (a Playwright measure at 360 px, `e2e/audit-log.test.ts`, 2026-11-03, #359).
