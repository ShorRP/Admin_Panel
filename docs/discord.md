# Discord

The reference behind `.claude/rules/discord.md`: what Discord was seen to do, what the fake copies of it, what the contract suite proves and what it costs to run, and where the application stands on Shor and on the test Discord server. The rule says what to do; this file says how it was known, with the ticket that learned each fact.

A fact below is **seen** when Discord did it in `pnpm test:contract` or in a probe, **documented** when a page of Discord's docs says it, and **inferred** when neither was tried. The `node_modules` citations are those of Vitest 5.0.5 and `discord-api-types`: an update of a package re-reads the citations that name it (`.claude/rules/dependencies.md`, **Docs that name a version**).

## The Discord seam

- Discord's change log of 2026-08-12 says that `GET /guilds/{guild.id}/channels` will "omit those channels from the response entirely", those that the application lacks View Channel on, from November 16, 2026, with no early opt-in over HTTP (https://docs.discord.com/developers/change-log, "Channel Obfuscation for Users and Bots", read 2026-11-05). The same entry says "Today, bots receive every channel in a guild, including ones they can't view", and on 2026-11-05 the route still listed them, with their overwrites: the test Discord server's private channel, which denies View Channel to @everyone and whose messages the application read with a 403, came back from it (a read-only probe of `GET /guilds/{guild.id}/channels` and of that channel's messages, #384). So `canViewChannel` reads a channel that `listChannels` does not give as not viewable.
- `Guild` keeps `id`, `owner_id` and `roles` whole, each role with its `permissions` as the bitfield string Discord sends, which with the roles of `listRoles` is all that `canViewChannel` needs of the Discord server. `GuildChannel` makes `id`, `type`, `name`, `parent_id` and `permission_overwrites` of `APIGuildChannel` required, since Discord sends all five for a channel of the Discord server (the contract suite reads them on the test Discord server, #384).
- `searchMessages` gives the messages flat, since Discord nests each message in an array that once held its context and now holds the message alone (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/message, **Search Guild Messages**, read 2026-11-05; the seven results of `probe-383-2.mjs` on the test Discord server came in seven arrays of one message each, 2026-11-05). Its query sends a list as an array, one parameter for each value (https://docs.discord.com/developers/reference, **Array Query Strings**, read 2026-11-05). What the search reads and serves, as the test Discord server answered, and what it costs, is in ADR 0010.
- `listMembers` needs the Server Members Intent: List Guild Members requires it (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/guild, read 2026-11-02), and it answered "Shor Panel Dev" `403 50001` before #318 turned the intent on.

## Errors and retries

- `getMember` returns `null` on Unknown Member, code 10007 (https://docs.discord.com/developers/topics/opcodes-and-status-codes, read 2026-10-23).
- A `202` of the search is retried after its `retry_after`, or after 0.25 s when that is shorter, since the docs ask for "a short delay" at 0 (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/message, **Search Guild Messages**, read 2026-11-05). No `202` was seen on the test Discord server, whose index was ready, in the ten probes and the three full runs of the contract suite of 2026-11-05, so the retry follows the docs alone.
- The search's 429s carried a `retry_after` of up to 4.4 s on the test Discord server (`probe-383-5.mjs`, 2026-11-05), over the 2 s the client waits, so a second search within seconds of the first can fail as Discord unavailable; the figures of the search's rate are in ADR 0010 and `docs/support-tickets.md`, **Why a record reads 100 archives**.
- Creating an AutoMod rule allows 5 in a row (`X-RateLimit-Limit`, reset after 6 s, 2026-10-25), past the 2 s the client waits, which is why the contract suite tries again after a 429, up to three times.

## The fake

- **State.** A member's arrival may be none, as Discord's `joined_at` may be (`node_modules/discord-api-types/payloads/v10/guild.d.ts:708`, #324). The list of members follows Discord's List Guild Members: its `limit` goes from 1 to 1,000, 1 by default, and its `after` is "the highest user id in the previous page" (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/guild, read 2026-11-02; #322, documented). It refuses no `limit`, since the panel always asks for 1,000 (inferred). The 403 of code 50001 that a test stands for the intent turned off with is what Discord answered « Shor Panel Dev » before #318 (#317, 2026-10-30, seen).
- **Outside the panel.** The events `MEMBER_UPDATE`, `MEMBER_KICK`, `MEMBER_BAN_ADD` and `MEMBER_BAN_REMOVE` are the ones Discord's docs name for a timeout, a nickname, a kick, a ban and its lifting, and their `user_id` is the "User or app that made the changes" (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/audit-log, read 2026-10-28, documented); the reason a member typed by hand is inferred. The audit log's `users` hold every account an entry's `user_id` or `target_id` names, as Discord's held the member a timeout targets (`pnpm test:contract`, 2026-10-29, seen); that it does for a kick, a ban and a former member is inferred (#182).
- **AutoMod's timeout.** AutoMod times out a member through a rule whose action is a timeout, for that action's duration, as `AUTO_MODERATION_USER_COMMUNICATION_DISABLED`, whose optional info holds the rule's name and trigger type (the audit log page cited above). That page says neither what such an entry's `user_id` holds nor whether AutoMod's timeout also adds a `MEMBER_UPDATE`, which a trial on the test Discord server answered: a keyword rule that timed out an account for 60 s left one such entry, whose `target_id` and `user_id` are both the member, the latter of which the panel does not read, whose `options` also hold `channel_id`, with no `changes`, so no end, and with a `reason` of Discord's own, "Message contained keyword defined in AutoMod rule", and no `MEMBER_UPDATE` (`GET /guilds/{guild.id}/audit-logs`, 2026-10-30, #209), which would have had the history and the audit log each show the timeout twice (inferred). The fake writes that entry and no `MEMBER_UPDATE`, for any rule, though only a keyword rule was tried, without `channel_id`, since no message is sent in a channel of the fake for a rule to act on.
- **AutoMod's limits.** 6 keyword rules per Discord server and, per rule, 1,000 keywords of up to 60 characters (ADR 0002, documented). Discord documents "150 characters" for the custom message and 2,419,200 seconds for the timeout (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/auto-moderation, read 2026-11-02), and the test Discord server measured otherwise for the first: a message of 150 characters, of 151 and of 300 was accepted, in a modify, and one of 301 was refused, in a modify and in a create, both with a 400 of code 50035, while 2,419,200 seconds were accepted and 2,419,201 refused, in a modify (`pnpm test:contract` and a throwaway probe of a rule's `custom_message`, 2026-11-02, #325, seen). The fake follows the 300 and not the 150, and the panel's own notices, of 120 and 118 characters, depend on neither. The 20 exempt roles stay the documentation's, which the test Discord server cannot try, since it probably holds fewer than 21 roles (inferred).
- **A timeout.** The owner, as Discord did, and a member with the Administrator permission, against whom Modify Guild Member "Will throw a 403 error" (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/guild, modified 2026-10-27), or whose highest role equals or exceeds the application's, since a bot acts only on "users whose highest role is lower than the bot's highest role" (https://docs.discord.com/developers/topics/permissions, modified 2026-08-13), are refused with a 403 of code 50013, whose code those two cases are inferred to share with the owner's, and an end more than 28 days ahead with the 400 of code 50035 (`pnpm test:contract`, 2026-10-28, seen). A timeout or its lifting is a `MEMBER_UPDATE` whose change `communication_disabled_until` holds the old end and the new one, the lifting no new one (the same run, seen).
- **A direct message** is refused with a 403 of code 50007, "Cannot send messages to this user", to a member who refuses them, and of code 50278, "Cannot send messages to this user due to having no mutual guilds", to an account that is not a member (https://docs.discord.com/developers/topics/opcodes-and-status-codes, modified 2026-10-27, documented; the status 403 is inferred), and one over 2,000 characters, the limit Discord documents for a webhook's `content` (`.claude/rules/monitoring.md`, **What an alert says**), with the 400 (inferred for a direct message).
- **Channels.** A channel lists its messages newest first, 50 a call by default, before a given message, as Discord's Get Channel Messages does (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/message, read 2026-11-04, documented), and fails as Unknown Channel, a 404 of code 10003, when the fake was not given it, as Discord answered a channel that does not exist (`pnpm test:contract`, 2026-11-05, #197, seen). It refuses no `limit` over 100, since the panel always asks for 100 (inferred). `listChannels` leaves no channel out, as Discord does today (see the first bullet of **The Discord seam** above).
- **The search** (#383). It never searches a direct message channel (inferred from the docs' name for the endpoint, a search of the guild). An ID it was not given is refused alone with a 403 of code 50001, Missing Access, as Discord answered a channel that does not exist (`pnpm test:contract`, 42 tests of 42, 2026-11-05, seen) and a channel the application cannot view, which `probe-383-6.mjs` (2026-11-05) searched alone and beside « transcriptions », where it was left out. It keeps the authors named, strictly after `min_id` and strictly before `max_id` (`pnpm test:contract`, 2026-11-05, seen), `limit` 1 to 25, 25 by default, and `offset` 0 to 9,975, and refuses a `limit` or an `offset` that is no integer and a text of more than 1,024 characters with the 400 of code 50035 that Discord answered (`pnpm test:contract` and `probe-383-10.mjs`, 2026-11-05, for `abc`, `NaN` and decimals, seen); `total_results` counts every page (seen). A text is its words, found in the content or in one text of an embed at a time, its title, its description, its author's name, its footer, or the name or the value of a field. `probe-383-6.mjs` and `pnpm test:contract` showed on 2026-11-05 that Discord finds a word whole, whatever its case and with its accents, never cut short, two words swapped or one apart in one text, and no two words across two texts of a message or two messages; `probe-383-9.mjs` (2026-11-05) showed it for the end of a field's value and the next field's name, a description and a footer, and two embeds. The rest of the fake's rule is inferred, not shown: that words split at everything that is no letter, digit or emoji, where a hyphen, a number, an ID and an emoji alone were shown (`probe-383-6.mjs`, `probe-383-7.mjs`, `probe-383-8.mjs`, 2026-11-05), and that two words are found within 2 words of one another, in any order, which follows the docs' `slop` of 2, "Max number of words to skip between matching tokens" (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/message, **Search Guild Messages**, read 2026-11-05), the fake's window being the words asked plus 2, where no probe tried a gap of more than one word. A blank text filters nothing and a text with no word in it finds nothing, as Discord answered an empty text, a blank one and `«»` (`probe-383-6.mjs`, 2026-11-05, seen). An apostrophe, U+2019, joins the letters around it into one word, so that « l’ouverture » is one word that « ouverture » does not find (`probe-383-6.mjs` and the contract suite, seen); a typed apostrophe of ASCII is not known to join (inferred, not tried). The fake has none of Discord's index lag, of tens of seconds (`probe-383-3.mjs` to `probe-383-9.mjs`, 2026-11-05), nor its 202 and its 429s.
- **The Message Content intent.** Without it, Discord gives the content and the embeds of a message of another author emptied, unless the application wrote it or it mentions the application, by `<@id>` or `<@!id>` (https://docs.discord.com/developers/events/gateway, **Message Content Intent**, read 2026-11-05, documented). That Discord's search with it off finds no text is inferred: the intent is on for both applications, so it was not tried (2026-11-05).
- **A kick** is a `MEMBER_KICK` under the application with the panel's reason, and the account stays as one that is not on the Discord server, as Discord did (trial under `pnpm dev`, 2026-10-29, #215, seen). It refuses the owner and a member whose highest role equals or exceeds the application's with the 403 of code 50013, though not one with Administrator, whom Discord's rule for a bot does not name, and answers an account that is not a member with Unknown Member (inferred from the same pages, since the kick stays out of the contract suite, #180).
- **A ban.** An account that is not on the Discord server is banned by its ID, as `MEMBER_BAN_ADD` under the application, with the panel's reason, which the ban keeps too, and the lifting is `MEMBER_BAN_REMOVE`, the account staying off the Discord server. A ban that deletes more than 7 days of messages, 604,800 seconds, is refused with the 400, and the lifting of an account that is not banned answers Unknown Ban, as Discord did (`pnpm test:contract`, 2026-10-29, #181, seen). A member is banned the same way, the member leaving the Discord server (the trial is in **The contract suite**, **What stays out**), and, like the kick, the owner and a member whose highest role equals or exceeds the application's are refused with the 403, though not one with Administrator (inferred from the pages the kick cites, since a member's ban stays out of the contract suite). A deletion of fewer than 0 seconds is refused with the 400, outside the 0 to 604,800 Discord documents (https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/guild, modified 2026-10-27), and the ban of an ID that names no account answers Unknown User (inferred, not tried). The fake deletes no message it holds.
- **An audit log reason** is documented as 1 to 512 characters (ADR 0004), and Discord refuses it neither way: it keeps a longer one's first 512 characters, and an empty one as no reason at all (`pnpm test:contract`, 2026-10-25, seen). The fake does the same.

## The HTTP adapter

A route the panel does not call is answered with Discord's own 404, `{"message": "404: Not Found", "code": 0}` (`curl https://discord.com/api/v10/no-such-route`, 2026-10-25).

## The real implementations

Vitest 5.0.5's fake timers do not fake `AbortSignal` (`FakeMethod`, `node_modules/vitest/dist/chunks/index.aTZaPVhf.js:3915`, whose list of faked methods holds no `AbortSignal`), which is why the delays run on real timers.

## The contract suite

- **Running it.** `vitest.contract.config.mts` loads `.env.local` through `process.loadEnvFile`, which leaves a variable the environment already sets alone (tried with Node 24.22.0, 2026-10-25). A worktree has no `.env.local`, and the suite run with the command of the rule's **Traps**, on the main checkout's values, passed on 2026-10-29 (#96, seen). The fake and the adapter pass the search's tests at once, and Discord takes about 2 to 3 minutes for the 9 of them (`pnpm test:contract` on the test Discord server, 2026-11-05, #383: 41 tests of 42 in 196 s, then 42 of 42 in 220 s and again in 165 s once every expectation was retried).
- **Bans.** It bans `CONTRACT_ABSENT_USER_ID`, an account absent from the test Discord server, by its ID, reads the reason back from the ban, lifts the ban, checks the entries the audit log keeps of both and that the audit log's `users` name that account, refuses a ban that deletes 7 days of messages and a second, and lifts the ban of an account that is not banned as Unknown Ban (#181). Before #178 gave "Shor Panel Dev" Ban Members, View Audit Log was enough to read the bans (`pnpm test:contract`, 2026-10-28, #176).
- **Members.** It lists the members, which needs the Server Members Intent that #318 turned on for « Shor Panel Dev » on 2026-11-02: the list, read 1,000 a call, holds the application and runs by ascending ID, and a page of one member followed by the page after its ID gives a member with a greater ID (`pnpm test:contract`, 2026-11-02, #322).
- **Timeouts.** It times out `CONTRACT_MEMBER_ID`, a member of the test Discord server below the application's role, replaces the timeout, lifts it, checks the entries the audit log keeps of them, which #177 reads, and that the audit log's `users` name that member, which #182 reads, gives that member 28 days less a minute and refuses 28 days and a minute, tries the owner, and sends that member a direct message, which it then deletes (#179). The 28 days was once exactly 28 days from the local clock: on 2026-11-02, its run for #246 answered the test "a timeout ends at most 28 days later" with a 400 of code 50035, while `date -u` read 08:56:27 and 08:56:28 around a `Date` header of 08:56:24 GMT from `curl -I https://discord.com/api/v10/gateway`, so that the clock ran 3 to 4 s ahead of Discord's, measured right after the run. A clock ahead of Discord's by any margin pushes an end of exactly 28 days past the limit (inferred), so the accepted end is 28 days less a minute, which a clock up to a minute ahead passes, and the refused one stays 28 days and a minute.
- **A channel's messages.** It posts to that member's DM channel a message with a text, then one that carries an embed alone, lists the channel's messages and reads them newest first, each with its author, the application, and its embed, then lists those before the newer one, and fails a channel that does not exist as Unknown Channel (`pnpm test:contract`, 31 tests of 31 on the test Discord server, 2026-11-05, #197). It uses a DM channel, as it did before the search tests found a channel of the Discord server (#383): it proves the route's shape, not the application's right to read the archives channel, nor that it receives the embeds of its own messages in a guild channel, which Message Content would empty for another author's (inferred from ADR 0009's reading of Discord's page on the gateway), and the manual try of #197 on Shor, whose record reads the archives through the search since #387, proves both for that read.
- **Roles and channels.** It reads the roles and checks that each carries a `colors.primary_color` that is a whole number from 0, which is no color, to 0xffffff, which the badge of a member's highest role reads, since the panel had never read `colors` before (`pnpm test:contract`, 2026-11-02, 31 tests of 31 on the test Discord server, #323). It reads the channels and checks that each carries an integer type, a name, a parent that is a listed category or none, and overwrites that are a role's or a member's, whose `allow` and `deny` are bitfield strings, and that each role carries its `permissions` as a bitfield string, listed or read with the Discord server, whose roles are the same, which `canViewChannel` reads (`pnpm test:contract`, 33 tests of 33 on the test Discord server, 2026-11-05, #384). The test Discord server held 4 categories on 2026-11-05 (a read-only probe).
- **The search** (#383). A nested `describe` posts six messages once, a content, an embed, both and three more, in the first text channel of the Discord server in which the application reads the history and posts, and deletes them at the end. It proves that a word of a content finds its message with its channel, its author and its content, that the title, the description, the author's name, a field's name and value and the footer of an embed find theirs, that a word is found whole, whatever its case, with its accents, and that an apostrophe, U+2019, joins the letters around it into one word (`pnpm test:contract`, 42 tests of 42 on the test Discord server, 2026-11-05, #386), that words close together in one text are found in any order and none across two texts, that the author filter keeps the application's messages and none of a member's, that `min_id` and `max_id` are strict, that `limit` and `offset` page newest first and `sort_order` reverses, and that a `limit` over 25, an `offset` over 9,975, a text of over 1,024 characters and a channel that does not exist are refused. Discord's index lags, a message being found 8 to 58 s after its post and its words later still (`probe-383-3.mjs` to `probe-383-9.mjs`, 2026-11-05), so each expectation of what a search finds is retried every 5 s for 4 minutes while it fails as an assertion, since a search can also give fewer messages than it should: the first full run of the suite, on 2026-11-05, got one message where two were expected for `min_id` and `max_id` (ADR 0010).
- **AutoMod.** It creates a rule that blocks the message with a custom message and times the member out, exempting the application's own role, which Discord accepts, reads it back as created, and checks the edges of what Discord measured (**The fake**): 2,419,200 seconds pass and 2,419,201 are refused through a modify, and a custom message of 300 characters passes while one of 301 is refused through a modify and through a create (#325). That rule is named `Contract …` and holds a keyword nobody types.
- **The application's `user_id`.** It checks that the audit log's entry of an AutoMod rule's creation, of a timeout's setting, and of the ban of an account absent from the Discord server and of its lifting carry the application's `user_id` (`src/discord/discord-api-contract.ts:354`, `:769`, `:858`, `:872`), which `attributionOf` reads (`.claude/rules/audit-log.md`, #210). That a timeout's replacement and lifting, a member's ban and a kick carry it too is inferred, since no check reads their `user_id` (`src/discord/discord-api-contract.ts`).
- **What stays out.** The fake has no other tests but the kick's and a member's ban, which stay out of the suite, like `DiscordOAuth`, since only the account can undo either, by joining again through an invite, once the ban is lifted (#181). For the kick, a trial under `pnpm dev`, on a test account that Gilles then brings back to the test Discord server, proves the rest (#180), as the first did on 2026-10-29, with `CONTRACT_MEMBER_ID`, whose direct message went 0.45 s before its `MEMBER_KICK` (#215). A trial in production proved it for a member's ban on 2026-10-29, where #218 had planned one under `pnpm dev`, of which the test Discord server's audit log holds no entry (Gilles, 2026-10-29, on #218; `GET /guilds/{guild.id}/audit-logs?action_type=22`, 2026-10-29). `DiscordOAuth` stays out of the suite, since a code exchange needs a member's consent in a browser: its fake follows Discord's docs, a Vitest test runs its real implementation over the adapter, and the first logins with "Shor Panel Dev", on `pnpm dev` and on a preview, prove the rest.

## Scanning a channel's messages

The reference behind `.claude/rules/discord-message-scan.md`, which `scanMessages` carries for the game log and the Bot log.

- A view makes from 1 to `MAX_SEARCHES` searches of 25 messages and stops once its page holds 25 lines: one search when Discord's answer is short or all of it passes the filters, as for an unfiltered page, a family or a text, and up to 4 when the filters drop lines, as for a combination that Discord cannot serve whole, such as an account with a channel (#395, #396). The 4 is the support tickets' window, against the rate limit of about one call every 4 to 5 s after a burst (ADR 0010; not measured on the pages that scan). What each page calls beside its searches is in `docs/game-log.md`, **Calls per view**, and `.claude/rules/bot-log.md`, **Reading**.
- `total_results` counts every page of a search with no bound (`src/discord/discord-api-contract.ts`, the test of `limit` and `offset`, seen) and was not shown under a `min_id` or a `max_id`, so that an older page whose total counts only what lies under its `max_id` is not proven, which is why « Plus anciens » does not read it (#394).
- A channel of exactly 25 lines, or a last page of 25, leads to an older page that says « Aucune ligne plus ancienne. », since a full page cannot tell that nothing older exists. And Discord's index lags and may give a page fewer than 25 lines though older ones exist (ADR 0010, which quotes the docs' warning that a search may return slightly fewer results than the limit): no « Plus anciens » then shows, and a line missing from a page is on no other, since the next page starts under the last line shown (inferred).
